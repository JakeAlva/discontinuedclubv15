That blue Crush can is Big Troppa Punch.

The bright blue can looks like a throwback. The flavor inside is tropical punch, not blue raspberry. Here is what to know about this limited-time Crush.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 5, 20264 minute read
The short answer

Big Troppa Punch is the Back to the 90s limited edition.

Crush Big Troppa Punch is a tropical punch soda sold under the Back to the 90s name. U.S. retail listings identify 12-ounce cans and 20-ounce bottles, and a distributor explicitly labels the release limited-time. We have not verified a firm national end date as of October 5, 2026.

What flavor is Crush Big Troppa Punch?

The U.S. can listing describes tropical punch, with a combination of sweet and tart fruit flavors. That is the useful starting point: the turquoise package is not a flavor code. Big Troppa Punch is distinct from Crush Orange and the earlier Electric Blue Razz edition.

The retro graphics are part of the product identity, not proof that this exact recipe was sold in the 1990s. We have not established a historical recipe match or tasted this release, so we are not presenting it as a revived formula or assigning it a review score.

Is it limited edition, and when does it leave?

PepsiCo Partners lists the 20-ounce bottle as an LTO, meaning a limited-time offering. Its description specifically says the product is available for a limited time. This is distributor evidence for the limited designation, not just social-media speculation.

Sporked reported the flavor in May 2026 with a planned July arrival. By our October check, retailer product pages are available. This article explains that 2026 release; it is not a claim that Crush launched a new flavor today.

The sources reviewed do not supply a verified nationwide final sale date. We therefore cannot promise a full fall run, say it ends on Halloween, or conclude that every can now for sale is discontinued stock. A limited launch can have an uneven retail footprint without either a permanent extension or a confirmed national withdrawal.

Where can you look for Big Troppa Punch?

Kroger has separate entries for 12-packs of 12-ounce cans and 20-ounce bottles. These are useful product references, but a listing is not a reservation or evidence that every branch carries the item.

Check the location attached to the search result. Delivery availability, pickup availability and a third-party shipping offer can be three different things. If one format is missing, search for the other before concluding that the flavor has disappeared. A bottle also lets you try it without committing to a full case.

Does Big Troppa Punch have caffeine or a zero-sugar version?

The can listing identifies it as caffeine-free and gives 160 calories and 43 grams of total sugars per 12-ounce can. The 20-ounce listing gives 270 calories and 71 grams of sugars per bottle. Those are different serving sizes, not conflicting figures.

The reviewed listings are for the regular sugar-containing soda. We did not verify a U.S. Big Troppa Punch Zero Sugar product. Crush Orange Zero Sugar is a different flavor, so a search result for that drink does not establish a zero-sugar version of this one. Check the package in hand for its final ingredient and nutrition information.

Big Troppa Punch versus Electric Blue Razz

The earlier Back to the 80s Electric Blue Razz is a blue raspberry soda. Big Troppa Punch uses Back to the 90s branding and a tropical punch identity. The decade and flavor printed below the Crush logo are the quickest way to distinguish them.

That matters when a store search bundles several flavors together or a social post says only new blue Crush. Match the actual label rather than the thumbnail color. We have not verified that the newer edition is a direct replacement for every Blue Razz package or market.

How to read this status

Limited-time is a release category, not a countdown. A can can remain on a shelf after the main promotion ends, while another store may sell through much earlier. Neither observation establishes a nationwide production date.

We are treating this as a documented limited release with current catalog entries, not declaring uninterrupted production or a permanent place in the Crush lineup. A dated manufacturer or distributor notice would be stronger evidence of an end than a single unavailable listing.

A better way to search than just blue Crush

Search for the full phrase Crush Big Troppa Punch, then try Crush Back to the 90s. Choose your store before checking pickup stock. For a phone inquiry, use the flavor name and package size rather than asking whether the store has the blue one.

Compare the total price and quantity before ordering from a specialty seller. A single-can listing and a 12-pack can use similar photographs. An ordinary grocery listing is worth checking before assuming that a limited label makes a collector-priced listing the only option.
