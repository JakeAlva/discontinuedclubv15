Mountain Dew's Trolli sequel is here. How long is it staying?

The orange bottles are real, and there is a Zero Sugar version. With October approaching, here is what the official rollout actually says about this candy collaboration.

By Discontinued Club ResearchChecked September 29, 20264 minute read
The short answer

This is a confirmed release, not a flavor rumor.

PepsiCo announced a U.S. run from August 24 through October 2026. Mango Pineapple Punch comes in regular and Zero Sugar versions. That limited window is not a guarantee that every store will have it throughout October.

What is Mango Pineapple Punch supposed to taste like?

The official regular product page describes mango and pineapple with a hint of strawberry. The name emphasizes two fruits, but the brand's description includes a third. That is the supported flavor profile, not our assessment of how strongly each comes through.

This is also a different product from the 2025 Cherry Lemon collaboration. PepsiCo identifies the new release as the brands' second year together. Searches for just "Trolli Dew" can therefore mix two releases. Look for the full Mango Pineapple Punch name before ordering an older listing or assuming a review describes this version.

When did it arrive, and when does the run end?

The August 24 announcement names U.S. retailers and an August-through-October window. It lists 12-ounce cans and 20-ounce bottles, plus regular 20-ounce bottles at participating Pizza Hut locations.

The release does not specify a final day in October. We would not turn that wording into an October 31 deadline or a promise of November stock. A seasonal distribution window and the date an individual shop sells its last bottle need not match.

Yes, there is Zero Sugar. No, that does not mean caffeine-free.

Mountain Dew has a separate official Zero Sugar listing. Check those exact words on the label; an orange bottle or a generic collaboration image does not identify the formula on its own.

The PepsiCo product-facts record for the 12-ounce Zero Sugar can lists 68 mg of caffeine per can. That figure belongs to the stated serving and formula. Do not apply it unchanged to a 20-ounce bottle, and use the label on the product you actually purchase if its formulation differs.

Is the soda itself spicy?

The launch pairs the drink with Trolli Spicy Crawlers. That is a candy pairing, not confirmation that the soda contains the candy's heat. The official drink description is fruit-led; we have not verified a spicy-soda claim.

It is worth separating the two because campaign artwork can show a drink and a snack as one experience. The ingredients and flavor of a companion product should not be transferred to the bottle beside it. Anyone looking for a replacement for Flamin' Hot Mountain Dew should not assume this is that recipe under another name.

Where should you look before paying a resale premium?

PepsiCo's business ordering catalog has a Mango Pineapple Punch listing for 20-ounce bottles in a 24-bottle case. That corroborates a distribution format. It is not a consumer price quote or evidence that all nearby convenience stores ordered it.

Start with current retail offers and confirm the selected store or delivery address. A marketplace seller may offer convenience or a different quantity, but a limited label alone does not prove that ordinary retail supply has ended. For shipped soda, also review the seller's condition photos and how damage claims are handled.

There is no need to confuse collecting a particular package with buying a drink to try. If the label design is the attraction, ask whether the listing photo shows the actual item. If the flavor is the attraction, prioritize the correct formula and a clear date label over a dramatic scarcity claim.

How to read this status

Limited release describes the announced sales program. It does not mean every remaining bottle becomes unavailable on the same day, and it does not establish that the flavor can never return.

A missing shelf tag at one store would tell us little about the national rollout. An explicit manufacturer update would be stronger evidence of an extension, another run or a future permanent listing.

Read the package, not just the orange label

Check the formula, container size and pack count together. A single bottle, a retail multipack and a wholesale case are not interchangeable offers. Compare the delivered price per container before choosing a seller, especially when a search result shortens the product name.

Discontinued Club does not currently stock this release. Our source links document the news; they do not promise that an order will be accepted or that a particular store has inventory. We have not sampled either formula.
