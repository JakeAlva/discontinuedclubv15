Dot's Chipotle Honey pretzels bring sweet heat to the fall lineup.

The yellow bag is a separate flavor, not a redesign of Original. Here is what the official listings tell us.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 15, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Chipotle Honey is a limited-time addition, not a replacement for Original.

Hershey introduced it in its September 16 fall campaign. Official product pages list the flavor in two bag sizes, and the brand still identifies it as limited edition at our October 15 check.

What is new about Chipotle Honey?

Hershey's September announcement positions Chipotle Honey alongside Original for fall football season. The described combination is honey sweetness and smoky chipotle heat over the brand's buttery seasoning. This is a new flavor offering, not an announcement that the core pretzel is disappearing.

We have not taste-tested it. In particular, the brand does not give a useful numeric heat scale in the sources we checked, so we would not label it mild or intensely hot based on the package alone.

Which bag sizes are listed?

Hersheyland has separate listings for a 16-ounce bag and a 5-ounce bag. Both identify Chipotle Honey as limited edition. The larger package is pictured here.

For price comparisons, divide the price by the number of ounces rather than comparing the two bag totals. The smaller bag can still be the sensible first purchase if you only want to find out whether you like the seasoning. Better value per ounce is not better value if most of a larger bag goes uneaten.

Check the full flavor name, too. Honey Mustard and Chipotle Honey are different choices; the presence of honey in both names is not enough to identify the seasonal product.

Is it already discontinued?

We do not have evidence to call it discontinued at this check. The current brand page promotes Chipotle Honey and links to its product listing. The dated announcement calls it a limited-time fall addition, without a fixed final sale date.

Limited edition describes the intended scope of an offer; it does not tell us the day the last bag will leave a store. An unavailable retailer page can be a local stock problem. Conversely, leftover bags after a campaign ends would not prove that production had become permanent.

How to find the right bag

Use the shopping link on the official product page as a starting point, then confirm the selected store and size. Before ordering a multipack, check whether its advertised quantity refers to bags or ounces.

If you are buying for a group, retain the ingredient and allergen label instead of serving from an unmarked bowl. For dietary needs, the current package is more useful than a generic description of the pretzel category.

How to read this status

We will only describe a permanent addition or a future return when there is evidence for it. A seasonal label alone establishes neither.

Independent coverage
