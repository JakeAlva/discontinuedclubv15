Alani Voodoo Vanilla is sold out online. Is it discontinued?

The small vanilla can has a big identification problem: it is not the same drink as Witch's Brew, and finding a Potion Pack does not mean you have found Voodoo Vanilla.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished September 28, 20264 minute read
The short answer

Sold out at Alani, but not confirmed discontinued.

On September 28, 2026, Alani Nu's own Voodoo Vanilla page offered a back-in-stock notification instead of a purchase option. We have not verified a discontinuation notice or restock date. That supports a narrow conclusion about the brand's online stock, not a nationwide disappearance.

What the official store actually shows

The official Voodoo Vanilla page remains published, with an unavailable offer and a notification signup. A page remaining online is useful for identification, but neither its presence nor its stock button tells us whether production is continuing.

There are several questions shoppers often collapse into one: can I order from the brand, can I find it locally, and is the product finished? Our check answers the first. We have not conducted a store-by-store inventory survey, so it would be misleading to turn this into a sold-out-everywhere headline.

What is Voodoo Vanilla?

Alani describes it as an Energy Elixir with whipped-cream and marshmallow flavor. The mini can holds 8.4 fl oz; its label lists 65 mg caffeine, 5 calories and zero sugar. The brand's online offer is a 12-pack, displayed at $24.99 during our check.

Elixir is part of the product's name, not a reason to mistake it for a caffeine-free coffee creamer. The front label and serving information are the useful identifiers. A smaller can also means a case cannot be compared with a full-size case by can count alone.

Does the Potion Pack include Voodoo Vanilla?

The standard Potion Pack on Alani's site lists six 12-ounce Witch's Brew cans and six 12-ounce Ruby Apple cans. It does not list Voodoo Vanilla as a third flavor. That is the pack composition to check when a search result groups the seasonal products together.

The brand describes Witch's Brew around green apple and Ruby Apple around red apple and caramel. Both are distinct from the vanilla Elixir. A retailer-created bundle may have different contents, but it needs an explicit list; do not infer an extra flavor from a collection name or an image.

Do not confuse the different cans' labels

The Potion Pack page lists 200 mg of caffeine per full-size apple-flavor can. That is a different amount from the mini Elixir. Alani also markets the products as possible mixing companions, which makes keeping the labels straight especially relevant.

Mixing two caffeinated products does not remove either one's caffeine. Follow the individual package directions and warnings; do not use a photograph of a mixed drink as a serving instruction. This article explains product identity, not a recommended recipe or caffeine intake.

Will Voodoo Vanilla be restocked?

We have not verified a dated replenishment announcement. The notification option is a way to hear about a change from the brand, not a reservation or a guarantee. It does not establish that the flavor will become year-round or that a particular retailer is expecting delivery.

A useful new piece of evidence would be a dated brand statement or a clearly identified store offer that can actually be fulfilled. Requests from fans, old launch posts and a high resale price may explain interest, but they do not provide a production schedule.

How to read this status

Sold out online is a dated observation about one sales channel. Discontinued is a conclusion about a product's continuing supply or place in the range. The first does not automatically establish the second.

We are not promising a comeback either. Keeping both possibilities open is more accurate than calling every unavailable drink retired or assuming that an email signup guarantees another production run.

A better way to check a listing

Match the front label, can size and number of cans before comparing prices. Search results can mix single cans, cases and multi-product bundles. A photo of several seasonal Alani flavors is not a substitute for the seller's written contents.

Discontinued Club is not offering Voodoo Vanilla in this report. This is an availability guide, not a restock announcement for our shop. We have not taste-tested the drink, and marketplace listings should not be read as evidence of a manufacturer's long-term plans.
