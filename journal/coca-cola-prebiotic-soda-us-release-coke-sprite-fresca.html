Prebiotic Coke is real. Where can you actually buy it?

The familiar red can has a new addition: fiber. But the announcement is a regional experiment, not an overnight change to every soda aisle in America.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 8, 20265 minute read
The short answer

A late-October test, not a nationwide launch.

Coca-Cola has confirmed a limited prebiotic-soda pilot in parts of New York, New Jersey and Pennsylvania beginning in late October 2026. It includes Coca-Cola Zero Sugar, Sprite Zero Sugar and Fresca. A nationwide release date has not been announced.

Where is prebiotic Coke launching?

The company's October 5 announcement calls this a regional test-and-learn. The purpose is to gauge consumer response, and the named states are not a promise of distribution to every store within them.

For a shopper outside the pilot area, the useful answer is not to assume the local supermarket missed a national launch. Distribution is deliberately limited. For a shopper inside it, a confirmed retailer and store-level listing will be more useful than a broad headline saying the drinks are coming to America.

The company is previewing its innovations at the October 7-9 NACS Show. A trade-show appearance and a retail release are different milestones: seeing the cans at an industry event does not establish that a nearby cooler has them.

Which flavors are in the lineup?

Coca-Cola's dedicated prebiotic page presents three familiar profiles: cola, Sprite's lemon-lime and Fresca's grapefruit-citrus. All three are advertised with 6 grams of prebiotic fiber and zero sugar. These are recognizable soda brands getting a separate formulation, rather than three unfamiliar fruit-soda names.

That distinction matters at the shelf. The large brand logo alone will not identify the new version. Look for the word Prebiotic on the front and compare the nutrition panel, especially when an online listing uses a shortened title or a generic brand photograph.

We have not tasted the drinks. Whether the added fiber changes body, finish or perceived sweetness is a question for an actual side-by-side tasting, not something that can be settled by the packaging.

What is in Coca-Cola Zero Sugar Prebiotic?

The official Coke product page lists a 12-fluid-ounce can with 15 calories, 7 grams of total carbohydrate, 6 grams of dietary fiber and 0 grams of sugar. It lists 34 milligrams of caffeine per can. Zero sugar therefore should not be read as zero calories or caffeine-free.

Soluble corn fiber appears in the ingredients. The listed sweeteners include aspartame and acesulfame potassium. Anyone choosing a soda by sweetener rather than flavor should read that panel instead of assuming a prebiotic label means the drink is unsweetened.

Those are product-label details, not our assessment of a health benefit. A beverage launch report cannot establish how a formulation will affect an individual reader.

How do Sprite and Fresca differ?

The Sprite product listing identifies its prebiotic version as caffeine-free. It also lists a 12-ounce serving with 15 calories, soluble corn fiber, aspartame and acesulfame potassium. Lemon-lime is the flavor distinction; the Coke caffeine figure should not be applied to it.

Fresca's U.S. page likewise describes its prebiotic soda as caffeine-free, in a 12-ounce size with 15 calories. Its ingredient panel includes concentrated grapefruit juice. The same page separately lists original Grapefruit Citrus, which is a useful reminder that the prebiotic version is not simply a new picture for the original product.

Compare the exact version you intend to buy. A search for a brand name can return regular soda, zero-sugar soda and a new fiber-containing version together, even when their labels and availability differ.

How to read this status

This is a confirmed test, not a confirmed permanent nationwide addition. There is no basis in this announcement to say the familiar products are being discontinued or universally reformulated. We will treat any later expansion as a separate development.

The practical shopping takeaway

Wait for a listing that identifies the exact prebiotic product and lets you check a real store or delivery area. Do not mistake the existence of an official product page for proof of local stock. Discontinued Club does not currently sell these drinks.
