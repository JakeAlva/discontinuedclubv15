Is Mountain Dew Pitch Black back in the U.S.?

The purple soda is making headlines again. This time, the announcement is for the UK, not a new American bottled or canned comeback.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 5, 20265 minute read
The short answer

The UK launch is real. A new U.S. retail return is not confirmed.

As of October 5, 2026, we have not verified an announcement of a new nationwide U.S. bottled or canned Pitch Black release. Carlsberg Britvic has announced a UK launch. That is good news for fans there, but it is not a U.S. release date or a guarantee of stock in American stores.

When is Pitch Black coming out in the UK?

Carlsberg Britvic's October 2 announcement sets out a two-stage schedule: an exclusive launch through wholesaler Booker in October, followed by a national UK rollout from November 2026. The format is a 500 mL price-marked bottle, supplied to the trade in cases of 12.

Booker is the wholesale route named in that announcement. A case specification for retailers is not a requirement that shoppers buy twelve bottles, and a rollout month does not promise that every shop will receive stock on its first day.

The announced flavor is citrus grape. Its dark purple appearance and space-themed packaging are part of the launch presentation. The statement does not establish a matching American rollout, a U.S. price, or a precise final sale date.

What did PEOPLE report?

Charna Flam's PEOPLE article, published October 2, 2026, covers the UK introduction and points to the manufacturer's release. Its product photograph is credited to Carlsberg Britvic. It is not an announcement of a new U.S. launch.

If a Facebook preview made you think Pitch Black was returning to your supermarket, the missing detail is the market. A post can reach American readers while describing a British release. The underlying article and the source it links matter more than where the headline appeared in your feed.

Why does Pitch Black sound like an American comeback?

There is real U.S. history behind the excitement. PepsiCo's July 2016 DEWcision announcement named Pitch Black the winner over Baja Blast and said it would join the permanent retail lineup that September. That was a statement about the 2016 plan, not a promise that the same assortment would remain unchanged forever.

PepsiCo later documented another relaunch in its April 25, 2023 prepared earnings remarks. The company described Pitch Black returning alongside a Zero Sugar option and a Pitch Black-flavored MTN DEW Energy drink. Those dated records explain why older return stories and several different packages still appear in searches.

There was also a different format: 7-Eleven's October 17, 2023 announcement promoted a limited-time Pitch Black Slurpee at 7-Eleven and Speedway. A frozen drink release is not a bottled soda rollout. That historical announcement does not verify what any particular store serves today.

Can you still find Pitch Black in the U.S.?

Possibly through an individual seller, but this report is not a live stock check. Import shops, resale listings and any remaining older inventory must be assessed separately. Finding a drink for sale in America does not tell you that it was produced for normal American distribution.

For a marketplace offer, distinguish the seller's location from the product's origin. A U.S. shipping address can belong to a business selling foreign-market bottles. Likewise, an old American product photo can remain attached to a listing after the original retail run. Neither situation proves a new national return.

Check the date on any comeback story before acting on it. A 2023 announcement can be accurate about 2023 and still be the wrong answer to a 2026 shopping question. The same country-and-format distinction matters when reading our Mountain Dew LiveWire guide.

Is the UK version the same as the old U.S. Pitch Black?

We have not established that the recipes are identical. A shared name and recognizable color are not a complete ingredient comparison. Until the exact labels are checked side by side, claims about an unchanged recipe, sweetness or identical taste would go beyond the evidence.

The historical U.S. Zero Sugar release also should not be read as confirmation of a new UK sugar-free option. The current announcement describes the bottle shown here; it does not announce a separate Zero Sugar variant. Readers with a specific ingredient preference should use the actual package rather than a label from a previous market or year.

How to read this status

U.S. return not confirmed is deliberately narrower than unavailable everywhere. We are answering whether there is a newly announced national American retail release, not claiming to have checked every cooler, marketplace seller or fountain machine.

We would change that answer with a dated U.S. brand announcement or other verifiable domestic distribution evidence. A social repost without a country, a legacy product page, or a bottle sold by an importer cannot establish that on its own.

Before paying for an imported bottle

Ask for the actual item photo, country of origin, bottle or can size, and printed date. Confirm whether the offer is one drink or a multipack. A familiar flavor name is not enough to identify the market or production run, especially when a listing uses a generic promotional image.

Compare the delivered price, including shipping, and read the seller's condition and returns information. Do not assume an expensive listing is newly made, rare, or identical to the version you remember. We have not verified any individual seller's inventory or tested the UK formula.
