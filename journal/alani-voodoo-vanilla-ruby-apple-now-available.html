Voodoo Vanilla and Ruby Apple are here. Pick your first taste.

From a fleeting Story to a real listing: two of the Alani flavors you asked about are ready to shop.

The short answer

No longer just coming soon.

As of November 2, Discontinued Club has live listings for Voodoo Vanilla Energy Elixir in an 8.4 oz single can and Ruby Apple in a 12 oz single can. These are separate unopened-can offers, not a two-flavor bundle. Open the product you want for the current price, availability and eBay checkout link.

Start with the flavor you are curious about

Shop Voodoo Vanilla Energy Elixir for the smaller 8.4 fl oz can. Alani describes the flavor around whipped cream and marshmallow. It is a different product from the apple flavors, not a vanilla edition of the Potion Pack.

Shop Ruby Apple for the 12 fl oz can. The brand's description points to red apple and caramel. Our Ruby Apple vs. Witch's Brew guide explains the distinction from the green-apple flavor. These are manufacturer descriptions, not our tasting notes.

Each listing is for one unopened can per purchased quantity. The side-by-side campaign image is a way to show the choices, not a promise that either listing includes both drinks. Read the written contents before ordering.

For the people who missed the post

Our October 29 announcement began with Jacob reporting that he saw these flavors on Alani's Story for 24 hours. We did not independently archive that Story or treat it as proof of a timed sale. Today's update has a simpler basis: our own listings are live.

We want more curious fans to have a chance at a first taste, including people who do not follow every drop or want a whole case of an unfamiliar flavor. Single-can offers give you a smaller starting point. They do not guarantee that every visitor will find stock, and we are not promising unlimited supply.

There is no need to buy both just because they share a banner. Choose the flavor that interests you, check the delivered total, and decide whether the offer works for you.

The product page has the current offer

These two new listings currently use eBay checkout. Follow the matching eBay link from the product page to confirm availability, shipping and the final total. This post is not a preorder, reservation or an automatic restock signup.

We have not verified the printed best-by date or full ingredient and allergen panel for either listing. If those details affect your choice, ask us for the actual label before purchasing. Follow the directions and warnings on the can you receive.

Still looking for the other apple flavor? Our Witch's Brew listings are separate offers with their own sizes and pack contents. Do not assume the new single-can format applies to those packs.

How to read this status
