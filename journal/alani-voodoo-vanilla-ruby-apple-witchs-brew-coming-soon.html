Alani's fall trio is coming to Discontinued Club.

Missed the Story? Still curious about the flavors? We are bringing Voodoo Vanilla, Ruby Apple and Witch's Brew to the shop.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 29, 20264 minute read
The short answer

Another chance to try the flavors you missed.

Discontinued Club is expecting Voodoo Vanilla, Ruby Apple and Witch's Brew. We want more of the people who missed these seasonal Alani flavors to have a chance to try them. This is an incoming-stock announcement, not a claim that the new batch is ready to order today. Arrival timing, prices and pack options have not been announced.

A short-lived Story, a longer-lasting wish list

Our founder, Jacob, reports seeing Voodoo Vanilla and Ruby Apple on Alani's Story for 24 hours. If a quick social post leaves you thinking about a flavor after the post disappears, we understand the feeling. That sighting prompted this update about what is coming to Discontinued Club.

We do not have an archived copy of that Story to independently check its wording or timing. We are sharing Jacob's observation, not calling it an official 24-hour sale, a nationwide restock or a promise that the flavors will stay around. Our own incoming-stock news is separate from whatever Alani may offer through its channels.

Vanilla, red apple and green apple

Voodoo Vanilla is Alani's mini Energy Elixir, described by the brand with whipped-cream and marshmallow notes. Ruby Apple is the red-apple-and-caramel option. Witch's Brew takes the green-apple-and-caramel direction. These are the manufacturer's descriptions, not our tasting scores.

All three are part of our incoming plan. We are not announcing a sampler, a particular can size for every incoming item or a purchase limit here. Those details belong on the actual listings once the shipment has arrived and we can show what is being offered. For help choosing between the apple flavors, read our Ruby Apple vs. Witch's Brew guide.

The goal is more first tastes, not more missed drops

Not everyone follows a brand account every day. You might hear about a flavor from a friend after the original post is gone, or only discover it when someone shares a photo of their haul. We want our shop to be another place to look, especially for people who are curious but have not had their first try yet.

That is a goal, not a guarantee of unlimited supply. We cannot promise that everyone will get a can or that every flavor will remain available together. What we can do is give readers an early heads-up without pretending that an expected shipment is already on the shelf.

Where to look when the batch arrives

Watch the current shop listings for the actual offer. A coming-soon article does not reserve stock, take payment or set a shipping date. Any older listing you see is a separate offer; check its own photos and details instead of assuming it represents this incoming batch.

Have a question about one of these flavors? Send us a message and name the flavor you are looking for. The contact form is a way to ask, not an automated restock alert or a place in a purchase queue. We would rather give you a clear answer than a countdown we cannot yet stand behind.

How to read this status

Coming soon describes Discontinued Club's expected inventory. It does not establish a new Alani production run, permanent availability or nationwide distribution. Stock and the purchase options for this batch will be determined by the live listings, not this announcement.

Before you order

Once a listing is live, check the specific flavor, can size, pack contents, printed date, condition and shipping information. Do not assume a reference photo is the exact package being sold. Follow the caffeine information and warnings on the actual can; this announcement is not a mixing recipe.
