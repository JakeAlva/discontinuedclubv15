Alani fall arrivals: Voodoo Vanilla and Ruby Apple are here.

What began as an incoming-stock announcement now has live listings. Our original Story context is preserved below.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 29, 2026Updated November 2, 20264 minute read
The short answer

Another chance to try the flavors you missed.

Update, November 2: Voodoo Vanilla Energy Elixir 8.4 oz and Ruby Apple 12 oz single cans are now listed at Discontinued Club. Our separate now-available post links to both offers. The October 29 incoming announcement below is historical context, not a preorder or a guarantee of continuing stock.

A short-lived Story, a longer-lasting wish list

Our founder, Jacob, reports seeing Voodoo Vanilla and Ruby Apple on Alani's Story for 24 hours. If a quick social post leaves you thinking about a flavor after the post disappears, we understand the feeling. That sighting prompted this update about what is coming to Discontinued Club.

We do not have an archived copy of that Story to independently check its wording or timing. We are sharing Jacob's observation, not calling it an official 24-hour sale, a nationwide restock or a promise that the flavors will stay around. Our own incoming-stock news is separate from whatever Alani may offer through its channels.

Vanilla, red apple and green apple

Voodoo Vanilla is Alani's mini Energy Elixir, described by the brand with whipped-cream and marshmallow notes. Ruby Apple is the red-apple-and-caramel option. Witch's Brew takes the green-apple-and-caramel direction. These are the manufacturer's descriptions, not our tasting scores.

All three appeared in our October 29 incoming plan. The new November 2 availability update covers the verified Voodoo Vanilla and Ruby Apple single-can offers. Witch's Brew has separate listings; this is not a sampler or a promise that every flavor comes in the same size. For help choosing between the apple flavors, read our Ruby Apple vs. Witch's Brew guide.

The goal is more first tastes, not more missed drops

Not everyone follows a brand account every day. You might hear about a flavor from a friend after the original post is gone, or only discover it when someone shares a photo of their haul. We want our shop to be another place to look, especially for people who are curious but have not had their first try yet.

That is a goal, not a guarantee of unlimited supply. We cannot promise that everyone will get a can or that every flavor will remain available together. The live listings now show what can actually be ordered, separately from our original heads-up.

Where to find the live offers

Open our now-available update for links to both new flavors, or browse the current shop listings. Each offer has its own contents and checkout terms. This article does not reserve stock or set a shipping date.

Have a question about one of these flavors? Send us a message and name the flavor you are looking for. The contact form is a way to ask, not an automated restock alert or a place in a purchase queue. We would rather give you a clear answer than a countdown we cannot yet stand behind.

How to read this status

Coming soon was the status of our October 29 announcement. The November 2 update confirms live store offers, not a new Alani production run, permanent availability or nationwide distribution. Check the current listings for stock.

Before you order

Check the specific flavor, can size, pack contents, printed date, condition and shipping information. Do not assume a reference photo is the exact package being sold. Follow the caffeine information and warnings on the actual can; this announcement is not a mixing recipe.
