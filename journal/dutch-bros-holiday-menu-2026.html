Dutch Bros opens the holiday menu with two new flavors.

A new month, a new seasonal menu. Here is the short version before your next drive-thru stop.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished November 2, 20262 minute read
The short answer

Two newcomers, plus familiar holiday names.

Dutch Bros announced its holiday menu on November 1. New Merry Magic and Sprinkle Cookie join returning Hazelnut Truffle and Candy Cane drinks, with availability while supplies last. November 2 is our reporting date, not a second launch date.

Fruit or cookie is the first choice

The official announcement describes Merry Magic as blackberry, peach and cherry with holiday candy. Its formats include Rebel, Myst Energy Refresher and lemonade. Sprinkle Cookie takes a sugar-cookie direction with Soft Top and festive sprinkles; options include Freeze, iced latte and iced chai.

The menu also adds a tree-shaped Holiday Sugar Cookie Cake Pop. For the returning drinks, think hazelnut and chocolate for Hazelnut Truffle, or peppermint and chocolate for Candy Cane.

Check the shop, not just the campaign photo

A campaign image can start a wish list, but it is not an order confirmation. Use the official location finder and the current menu for the shop you plan to visit. Ask about the specific drink, format and toppings you want rather than assuming every customization in a social photo is standard.

Compare the total for your actual order. A drink name alone does not tell you the price of a chosen size or an extra topping. For ingredients and dietary questions, consult the brand's nutrition information and the staff instead of treating the color or name as an ingredient list.

We have not taste-tested these drinks or visited a shop for this report. There is no invented ranking here: this is a starting point for deciding what to investigate, not a verdict about what you will enjoy most.

If you are planning a group stop, have each person choose a base and size before placing the order. A shared flavor name can hide very different orders. Keep the receipt or order summary so you can identify the version you liked if you decide to return.

A first try can simply be one drink that sounds appealing. There is no need to order the whole lineup to participate in the season. Save your own notes about the exact version you chose rather than relying on a broad flavor name later.

How to read this status

Seasonal is not the same as discontinued. We are following a current menu announcement, not documenting a permanent retirement or a future return schedule.
