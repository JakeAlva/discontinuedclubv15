Taco Bell's Milk Bar Birthday Cake Empanada is here.

The birthday-cake collaboration returns in a different shape. Here is what to know before ordering the new limited-time dessert.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 16, 20264 minute read
The short answer

The empanada launched October 15 at participating U.S. restaurants.

Taco Bell announced a limited-time Milk Bar Birthday Cake Empanada with a $3.49 advertised price, subject to local pricing and availability. It is a new dessert format, not a return of the earlier Birthday Cake Churros.

What is inside the Birthday Cake Empanada?

The official menu description identifies a crisp pastry shell around birthday-cake-batter filling, finished with pink frosting and rainbow sprinkles. It is an assembled restaurant dessert, not a slice of Milk Bar cake or a packaged grocery snack.

The photograph helps explain the format, but we have not eaten it. We cannot yet tell you how crisp it stays after delivery, how sweet the filling tastes or whether it is worth buying again. Those are review questions, not facts that a product announcement can answer.

How much does it cost?

Taco Bell's featured menu displayed $3.49 and 290 calories for the item at our check. Treat that as the public menu reference, not a guaranteed total at every restaurant. Select your location and ordering method before comparing prices.

The difference between an advertised price and the checkout total matters especially on a small dessert order. Tax and delivery-related charges can change the amount you actually pay. Our page is not a live quotation for your address, and we are not reproducing a local cart.

For ingredient or allergen questions, use the nutrition information connected to the exact menu item. A description such as birthday cake gives you a flavor idea; it is not a full ingredient list. Do not reuse the information for the older churros merely because the collaboration name is familiar.

Can you get it as a birthday reward?

The published offer lets eligible Rewards members choose the empanada instead of a regular Baja Blast Freeze from October 15 through October 28, while supplies last. It requires a birthday on the profile and redemption through Taco Bell's app. It is not a free item for every customer.

Look at the reward actually issued to your account before planning a visit around it. The terms limit it to one reward per transaction and restrict combinations. A screenshot of someone else's reward does not establish that it is available to you.

The October 28 date is the empanada reward window, not a confirmed last day for every restaurant to sell the dessert. This is an easy detail to lose when a deal gets summarized in a social post. Keep the purchase availability and promotional eligibility as separate questions.

Is this the same Milk Bar dessert as last time?

No. The February 20, 2025 announcement described Birthday Cake Churros: two churros with birthday-cake frosting inside and confetti sprinkles outside, advertised at $2.99 during that limited offer. That historical price and serving format belong to the churros, not this empanada.

That is why an old review can be both genuine and unhelpful for the new item. Someone praising the churros' texture or comparing their two-piece serving with another dessert has not reviewed the pastry now on the menu. The familiar brand partnership is continuity; the dessert itself is the change.

For readers keeping track of discontinued favorites, this is a useful example of a flavor theme returning without the original item returning. A birthday-cake filling can appear in another format while the thing you remember remains a separate product with its own history.

How long will the empanada be around?

We have not verified a universal final sale date. The launch is described as limited time and subject to supplies. That does not establish a permanent menu addition, and it does not mean every participating restaurant will run out together.

Check your selected restaurant before making a special trip, especially if the dessert is the only reason you are ordering. If it disappears from one local menu, the next useful question is whether that location is temporarily out or has ended its offer. Neither answer should be generalized nationwide without more evidence.

Our Dunkin Berry Battie and bat sleeve guide makes a similar distinction between a menu product and a separate promotion. Keeping those dates apart makes these short-lived releases much easier to follow.

How to read this status

Limited time is a release designation, not a verified discontinuation date. If an end date is announced later, it should be added as a dated update rather than guessed from a rewards calendar.

Reporting, not a taste review

Discontinued Club does not currently sell this dessert. This article covers the menu change and ordering details from public sources. We have not received a sample, checked a local kitchen or ranked it against Milk Bar's bakery products.
