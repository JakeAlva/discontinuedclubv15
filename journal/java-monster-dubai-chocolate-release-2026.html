Java Monster Dubai Chocolate is real. Here is what is in the new can.

Monster is taking chocolate and pistachio into its coffee aisle. This one has an official announcement, not just a viral mockup.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 12, 20264 minute read
The short answer

Yes, Dubai Chocolate is an official new Java Monster flavor.

Monster announced an October 2026 arrival for Java Monster Dubai Chocolate. Its U.S. product page lists a 15-fluid-ounce can with 200 mg of caffeine, 220 calories and 8 grams of protein. The drink belongs to the coffee-and-cream Java range, not the Ultra Zero Sugar line.

When does Java Monster Dubai Chocolate come out?

Monster's October 1 announcement gives October 2026 as the arrival window. The flavor also has a live official U.S. product page, so this is more than a leaked name or a fan-designed can.

The announcement reviewed does not give one nationwide on-sale day. That matters if you are planning a store trip: a month-long rollout window is not the same thing as simultaneous availability at every location.

A missing spot in your local cooler would not contradict the launch by itself. Ask for the complete product name when checking with a store, and confirm that its inventory result refers to this flavor rather than another Java Monster.

What flavor is Dubai Chocolate Monster?

Monster describes coffee and cream with real cocoa, plus pistachio and thin-phyllo-inspired flavors. It is a dessert-inspired coffee drink. The name does not mean the can contains a chocolate bar, and flavor language should not be read as a promise of pastry pieces or crunch.

We have not taste-tested it. The brand description explains the intended profile; it cannot tell us how strongly each note comes through, how sweet it seems or how it compares with your usual coffee. Those judgments need an actual tasting.

For a first try, a single can is a more useful experiment than ordering a case on the strength of the name. Check the format carefully when comparing listings: a coffee-and-cream drink is a different purchase from a fruit-flavored energy drink.

How much caffeine is in the can?

The official U.S. listing gives 200 mg of caffeine per 15-fluid-ounce can, alongside 220 calories and 8 grams of protein. Those figures apply to this product and size, not to every drink carrying the Monster logo.

It is especially easy to miss the serving size when a shopping result displays only a cropped front label. Match the product name, can volume and full nutrition panel before comparing it with another Java or an Ultra flavor.

For ingredient or allergy questions, use the current complete package label. A description mentioning pistachio, tahini or phyllo is not a substitute for an ingredient list and allergen declaration. We are not inferring nut-free, gluten-free or dairy-free suitability from the promotional wording.

Where should you look for it?

Start with Monster's store locator, then confirm the exact flavor with the retailer. Search results and nearby-store suggestions are useful leads, but they are not our independent verification of live shelf stock.

Online, compare the quantity selected and the delivered total. A case price can look like an expensive single can if a marketplace title leaves out the count. Check whether the seller shows the actual packaging and whether the order is available now or merely accepting advance purchases.

Discontinued Club does not currently sell Dubai Chocolate. The official links here are reporting and shopping references, not affiliate links or a promise that we have inventory.

Is it limited edition or replacing another Java?

The sources reviewed introduce a new flavor but do not establish an end date or identify it as a replacement for another Java product. We are therefore not labeling it limited edition, permanent forever or the direct successor to a discontinued flavor.

A new launch and a separate discontinuation can happen near each other without being a one-for-one replacement. Our Monster flavor timeline tracks the historical side; this report covers the new can on its own evidence.

How to read this status

Confirmed U.S. launch refers to the official October announcement and current U.S. product page. It does not verify availability at every retailer or establish how long the flavor will remain in production.

Before buying

Check the full flavor name, 15-fluid-ounce size, can count, delivered price and current label. There is no reason to treat an announced launch as a scarce discontinued collectible simply because early listings are expensive.
