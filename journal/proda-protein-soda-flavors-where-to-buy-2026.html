What is PRODA? The protein soda behind the new celebrity headlines.

The celebrity news is new. The soda is already on sale. Here is what to know before ordering a case.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 14, 20263 minute read
The short answer

PRODA is a carbonated protein drink, not a new energy drink.

PRODA markets a 12-fluid-ounce soda containing 10 grams of whey protein isolate and no sugar. The October 13 news concerns Justin Timberlake, Jessica Biel and Joey Gonzalez joining as co-founders; it is not an announcement that the first cans go on sale that day.

What happened on October 13?

In its official announcement, PRODA said the new co-founders would help develop the business, including its creative direction and future products. It described a brand already selling through Sprouts, Amazon and its own website.

That distinction is worth preserving when a familiar face makes an unfamiliar drink suddenly appear everywhere in your feed. This is business news about an existing product, not evidence of a newly invented flavor, a sellout or a limited-edition drop.

Which PRODA flavors are available?

The current brand catalog names seven: Classic Orange, Cherry Lime, Strawberry Lemonade, Lemon Lime, Golden Apple, Root Beer and Shirley Temple. That gives shoppers a choice between familiar soda profiles and fruit-led combinations.

We have not tasted these drinks, so this is not a ranking. A flavor name tells you the intended direction, not whether the finish, carbonation or sweetness will suit you. For a first purchase, a single can or a clearly described variety pack is a lower-commitment way to compare them than seven separate cases.

Is PRODA caffeinated? What protein does it use?

The Classic Orange product page identifies whey protein isolate and labels the drink caffeine-free. It is not being sold as a caffeinated energy drink or a creamy bottled shake.

Do not let the word soda substitute for an ingredient check. A protein formula deserves the same full-label review as any other food purchase, particularly when buying for someone with dietary restrictions. Zero sugar does not, by itself, tell you everything about the ingredients or suitability.

Likewise, adding protein to a drink is a product specification, not proof that it is the best choice for every person. This guide explains the package; it does not make a health or performance recommendation.

Where can you buy it in the U.S.?

PRODA lists Sprouts, Amazon and its own store as shopping routes. Confirm the selected flavor and local stock before making a trip. A national retailer relationship is not a live inventory count for your nearest branch.

At the brand store, pay attention to the purchase selector: a subscription and a one-time order are different commitments. Compare the delivered total and can count, not just the price beside the first button. This is especially important when comparing a single-flavor case with a mixed pack.

How to read this status

Current U.S. release means there is official product and distribution information. It does not promise that every flavor is stocked everywhere, or that the lineup will never change.

Our takeaway

Shop the drink on its label and flavor, not on an assumption that celebrity coverage makes it rare. Discontinued Club does not currently sell PRODA; these are independent reporting links, not our stock listings.
