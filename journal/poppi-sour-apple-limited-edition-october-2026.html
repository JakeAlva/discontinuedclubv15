Is Poppi Sour Apple limited edition? What its October window means.

The green can is still being promoted for fall. That is different from a promise that it is joining the permanent lineup.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 15, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Yes, it is limited edition. It is not confirmed discontinued today.

Poppi labels Sour Apple a limited edition, and PepsiCo gives an end-of-October availability window subject to supplies. Its official product page remains live at our October 15 check.

How long is Poppi Sour Apple available?

In its September 18 back-to-school guide, PepsiCo said the flavor would be available through the end of October, while supplies last. Its October 9 Halloween roundup continued to feature Sour Apple as a seasonal option.

Our reading is that this is a stated seasonal window, not a precise national shelf-clearance date. A store could run out sooner; another could have remaining cans afterward. Neither situation, by itself, demonstrates a permanent lineup change.

That matters if you found this page after seeing an empty shelf. The most useful immediate question is whether your store has sold through its allocation, not whether every can in the country disappeared on the same day.

What flavor is the green Poppi can?

PepsiCo describes a green-apple profile with candy-like sweetness and a sour finish in its Halloween article. We have not tasted it, so that description should not be read as a Discontinued Club review or a comparison with a specific candy.

The official product page lists 35 calories, 5 grams of total sugar and 3 grams of fiber per can. Its ingredients include cane sugar and stevia leaf extract: this is not a zero-sugar drink or an unsweetened water. Use the can you purchase for the current nutrition and ingredient information.

Where should you look before paying a resale premium?

Start with Poppi's product page and store finder, then check the retailer with your location selected. We have not verified local inventories, so a national listing should be treated as a lead, not a reservation.

For an online listing, compare the number of cans, their volume, shipping and total price. A twelve-pack should not be compared directly with the price of a single can, and a sold-out message from one seller is not proof that a resale premium is justified everywhere.

If you are trying it for the first time, a single can where available is a more cautious purchase than a full case bought only because a flavor is seasonal. Scarcity is not a substitute for knowing whether you enjoy the drink.

For a reader tracking the flavor over time, keep the seasonal window and the date of the stock check together. A screenshot without a date quickly loses its usefulness, especially when an old search result remains visible after a promotion has ended.

How to read this status

We keep seasonal availability separate from discontinuation. A future permanent release, extension or return would need fresh evidence; this October guide does not predict one.
