Little Debbie adds Hot Cocoa Mini Muffins to the Christmas lineup.

Two new additions join the familiar holiday snacks. The release window matters more than a single nationwide shelf date.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 14, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Hot Cocoa Mini Muffins are an official 2026 holiday addition.

McKee Foods announced the muffins and Big Pack Christmas Old Fashioned Donuts on October 13. Its U.S. rollout window runs from mid-October through mid-December 2026. This is a staggered seasonal release, not a promise that every store already has both products.

What are Hot Cocoa Mini Muffins?

Little Debbie's product page describes cocoa-flavored mini muffins with marshmallow inclusions. The pictured family carton contains 20 muffins in five pouches, with four in each pouch.

That count is useful when comparing shopping results: 20 muffins does not mean 20 individually wrapped packs. Match the carton size and pouch count before comparing prices, especially if a marketplace groups several boxes into one listing.

We have not tasted the new muffins. The name and official description identify the intended flavor; they do not establish how closely the snack resembles your preferred hot chocolate.

What else is new, and what is coming back?

The 2026 announcement also introduces Big Pack Christmas Old Fashioned Donuts: glazed cake-style donuts with red and green sprinkles. Christmas Tree Cakes are returning favorites, not a newly invented 2026 product.

The seasonal catalog separates multiple package formats, including family, Big Pack, single-serve and club offerings. It also lists Christmas Tree Brownies, Gingerbread Cookies, North Pole Nutty Buddy bars and Cherry Cordials.

For a collector or a shopper trying to replace a favorite, the distinction is important. An unfamiliar box size can be new without the underlying snack being new. A familiar holiday treat returning to shelves is not evidence of a permanent relaunch.

When should you start looking?

The announcement gives a mid-October to mid-December rollout across grocery, mass-merchandise and convenience retailers. The broader seasonal catalog describes a mid-October to late-December availability season. Those statements describe different things: when products roll out and the general holiday selling period.

Neither is a guarantee that a particular store receives every item or keeps it through a specific day. Check the exact product with your retailer before driving across town. A blank shelf during rollout does not settle whether a product has been discontinued.

Do not confuse the new flavor with an older recipe

Little Debbie also has a Mini Muffin Hot Cocoa Mugs recipe. That project starts with Chocolate Chip Mini Muffins and decorates them to resemble cups. It is a different use of the words hot cocoa, not the product shown above.

When searching, include the full packaged-product name and inspect the front of the box. A recipe page can answer how to make a holiday treat without telling you anything about the new flavor on a store shelf.

How to read this status

Seasonal release means it is being offered for a holiday selling period. It is not a promise of year-round availability, and we have not assigned an exact last-production date.

Before buying a holiday multipack

Check the package date, pouch count and full ingredient label on the actual box. Discontinued Club does not currently sell these snacks. Our coverage is a buying reference, not a stock alert or a reason to pay a resale premium during a new rollout.
