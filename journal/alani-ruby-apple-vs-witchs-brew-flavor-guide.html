Ruby Apple vs. Witch's Brew: which apple are you after?

Similar cans do not mean the same flavor. Start with the apple, then check the actual listing.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 29, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Red apple or green apple is the useful starting point.

Alani describes Ruby Apple as the sweeter red-apple-and-caramel counterpart to Witch's Brew, whose profile centers on green apple and caramel. That is a comparison of the brand's descriptions, not a blind taste test or a claim that one is better. Choose by the flavor direction that interests you, not just by the purple packaging.

Use the description as a starting point, not a verdict

The official Potion Pack description presents Ruby Apple as the red-apple option, while the Witch's Brew page identifies its green-apple profile. Both mention caramel. Sharing that note does not make Ruby Apple a renamed Witch's Brew.

Think about what you are hoping to discover. Are you looking for a familiar seasonal favorite, or are you curious about the other apple interpretation? Either is a reasonable reason to choose a can. A flavor name can help narrow the choice, but it cannot tell you exactly how sweet, tart or intense the drink will seem to you.

We have not run a side-by-side tasting for this guide, so there is no invented winner, score or tasting note. A useful recommendation should make the choice clearer without borrowing somebody else's reaction and presenting it as our own experience.

A product photo is not a packing list

The two-can image above is a reference for identifying the flavors. It does not mean that every listing includes both, or that buying a single quantity will deliver the pictured assortment. On any retailer's page, the written contents and the quantity selected matter more than a collection photo.

Check the flavor name, volume per can and number of cans in one purchased unit. Then compare the full delivered price, including shipping, with the amount you actually want. A larger pack is not automatically a better first purchase if your main goal is simply to find out whether you enjoy the flavor.

For the separate size question, our Witch's Brew full-size vs. Mini guide covers the labels. Do not use packaging color or can height alone to infer serving information. Read the package you will actually receive.

Both apple flavors are in our incoming plans

Discontinued Club is expecting Ruby Apple and Witch's Brew alongside Voodoo Vanilla. Our coming-soon announcement explains that plan separately from this flavor comparison. It does not open preorders, announce pack sizes or promise a date.

When you are ready to look, use the current shop to see what is actually listed. An article can help you decide what to try; only the live offer can tell you what is available to buy. You can also ask us about the flavor you want without treating that conversation as a reservation.

How to read this status

Seasonal flavor comparison describes the scope of this guide, not a guarantee of stock or a permanent place in the lineup. A sold-out retailer page and a discontinued product are different claims. This article does not establish that either flavor has been permanently retired.

A first-try checklist

Match the exact name on the can to the listing title, then check the package contents, printed date, condition and shipping terms. If an important detail is missing, ask before ordering. For a flavor you have never tried, the smallest suitable offer may make more sense than buying extra simply because a larger pack exists.

Follow the caffeine information and warnings on the actual package. This is a shopping and flavor-identification guide, not advice to combine caffeinated drinks or a claim that any particular serving is suitable for everyone.
