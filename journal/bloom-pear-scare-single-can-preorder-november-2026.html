Bloom Pear Scare: a single-can preorder for the flavor you missed.

The pear-and-cinnamon can is coming to our shelf. Here is what you are reserving, when we expect to ship it, and what happens if the incoming delivery changes.

The short answer

One can. A clear preorder. No case required.

Discontinued Club is taking preorders for one unopened 12 fl oz Bloom Pear Scare can at $7.99 per can, with shipping and applicable tax calculated at checkout. Payment is collected when you order. We expect to ship on November 30, 2026, provided the incoming delivery arrives as expected by November 28. This is an expected shipping date, not a guaranteed delivery date.

What is Pear Scare?

Pear Scare belongs to Bloom's Sparkling Energy range. The product-label information reproduced by Food Lion describes pear with cinnamon. That makes it a different fall-flavor idea from caramel apple: the stated pairing is fruit and spice, not caramel. We have not tasted this batch and are not assigning it a sweetness score or promising that it will taste exactly like a dessert.

The full-size label lists 180 mg of caffeine per 12 fl oz can. Bloom also makes other beverage formats, so check the exact product rather than treating every Bloom can as interchangeable. Our preorder is the full-size Sparkling Energy can, not an Energy Mini, a powder, or Bloom Pop soda. Read the can's ingredients and warnings before drinking it.

For someone who enjoys comparing seasonal flavors, that distinction is the interesting part. A familiar autumn ingredient does not mean an identical drink. Pear Scare is worth considering because it has its own stated flavor combination, not because we can promise it will replace a favorite from another brand.

Why offer it by the can?

Sometimes you want to find out what a flavor is like, not commit to a whole case. Our listing makes that choice straightforward: quantity one means one sealed can. It does not mean one twelve-pack. You can check the available quantity on the product page without relying on an inventory number printed in an article that may be out of date tomorrow.

A single-can purchase also deserves an honest price comparison. The item price is not your delivered total. Shipping can be a meaningful part of a small order, so review the complete checkout amount before deciding. If you already know you want a case, compare the pack size and full delivered cost with other sellers. Our offer is the ability to reserve an individual can; we are not claiming the lowest price per ounce.

The two dates that matter

November 28 is the latest arrival date currently shown for our incoming delivery. November 30 is our expected ship date to customers. Those are different steps: the delivery must reach us, then we need to inspect the cans and pack the orders before handing them to the carrier. Neither date is a promise that a package will be at your door that day.

Please do not use this preorder for an occasion that requires guaranteed delivery by a specific date. The schedule depends on the incoming shipment, and transit to your address comes afterward. If the delivery is delayed, we will contact affected buyers with an updated date and cancellation/refund options. We would rather explain a change than leave you wondering whether an order has gone missing.

How ordering works

Open the Pear Scare product page, choose your quantity, and review the preorder notice before checkout. Your payment is collected now, not on the expected ship date. Keep the order confirmation so that you can refer to the purchase if you need help.

Pear Scare must be ordered separately from ready-to-ship items. The website does not combine this preorder with in-stock products in one cart. That keeps a normal order from being held while we wait for the incoming cans and keeps the preorder schedule clear. Separate orders may each have their own shipping charge; check the total shown for each.

Need to cancel before shipment? Email jacob@discontinuedclub.com with your order reference. Do not send card details. If you notice an address error, get in touch promptly so we can review it before a shipping label is used.

What we will check when the cans arrive

The photograph identifies the product design; it is not evidence of the condition or printed date of an unseen shipment. We will inspect the incoming cans and check their best-by dates on arrival. We are not publishing a batch date we have not verified. If a particular date or display-perfect condition matters to you, contact us before ordering.

This announcement does not mean Pear Scare is permanently discontinued, exclusive to Discontinued Club, or impossible to find elsewhere. It means we have opened a specific preorder with a stated price and shipment plan. The goal is simple: give another person who missed a flavor a chance to try one, with the important details visible before checkout.
