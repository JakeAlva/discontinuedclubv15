Ocean Spray Cranberry Cider joins the fall drink lineup

Cranberry, apple and warming spice notes in a bottle that can be served warm or chilled. The fall rollout is underway, but local stock still needs checking.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 21, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Cranberry Cider is a seasonal juice drink, not hard cider.

Ocean Spray announced the limited-edition drink in September for an October 2026 rollout. The 60-fluid-ounce bottle is labeled Cranberry Cider and identifies the contents as a cranberry-and-apple-flavored juice drink.

What is the flavor?

Ocean Spray's September 23 announcement describes cranberry and apple flavors with cinnamon, clove and ginger notes. The brand suggests serving the drink warm or chilled. We have not tasted it, so that description is the manufacturer's positioning rather than a sweetness or spice rating from us.

The official product page and bottle artwork are useful for identifying the release. Look for the full Cranberry Cider name and the limited-edition strip, rather than choosing the first red Ocean Spray bottle in a search result.

The wording matters: a flavored juice drink is not automatically 100% juice, fresh-pressed cider or an alcoholic beverage. Read the specific bottle instead of borrowing nutrition or ingredient information from another cranberry product with a similar color.

Where is Cranberry Cider being sold?

The announced rollout begins in October through Amazon, Walmart.com and select Walmart stores. Select is an important qualification: the announcement does not promise every store, every ZIP code or a single nationwide shelf date.

A Walmart Business listing also identifies the 60-fluid-ounce bottle. Treat that as a product reference, not proof that an individual consumer order or pickup is available in your area.

When checking a listing, compare the bottle count and total volume before comparing prices. A case can appear beside a single bottle, and delivery charges can change which option is actually cheaper. Set your delivery or pickup location before making a trip.

This is an October shopping guide for a previously announced product. We are not presenting the date at the top of this article as the date the brand first revealed it.

How much is in one bottle?

For planning purposes, 60 fluid ounces divides into six 10-ounce pours or seven full eight-ounce pours with four ounces left over. Those are our volume calculations, not a recommendation to replace the serving size printed in the nutrition panel.

For a side-by-side tasting, pour a small chilled sample and a separately warmed sample, then compare them yourself. Keep the original bottle nearby so guests can check ingredients. Follow its storage directions; a seasonal serving suggestion does not replace the package instructions.

If you want to document the release for your collection, photograph the front, back and date code. A clear picture of the exact bottle will be more useful later than a screenshot that groups several products together.

How to read this status

Ocean Spray calls this a limited edition, but the reviewed announcement does not give a final production month or last retail sale date. We will not convert fall positioning into an invented Thanksgiving or December cutoff. Remaining inventory can differ from store to store.
