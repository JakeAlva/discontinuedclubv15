Witch's Brew: full-size or Mini? Read the can before you buy.

Same green-apple-and-caramel flavor direction, different cans. The smaller Witch's Brew is not just a shorter version of the full-size label.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 25, 20264 minute read
The short answer

12 oz has 200 mg of caffeine. The 8.4 oz Mini has 100 mg.

Alani Nu's official 2026 pages list a 12 fl oz Witch's Brew with 200 mg of caffeine and an 8.4 fl oz Mini with 100 mg. Both are seasonal products, not evidence of permanent discontinuation. This October 23 Friday-edition guide was published and checked October 25, ahead of Halloween.

The label difference is bigger than the can height

The full-size product page identifies a 12 fl oz serving, 15 calories, zero sugar and 200 mg of caffeine per can. The Mini page shows an 8.4 oz format, 10 calories, zero sugar and 100 mg of caffeine. Those are the manufacturer's current label figures, not a promise that every older package has an identical ingredient panel.

The arithmetic is useful: the Mini has 70% of the fluid volume but half the caffeine of the 12 oz version. Do not estimate caffeine from can height or assume that a smaller container is caffeine-free. Compare the amount per can on the package you are actually buying.

Both official pages describe a green-apple-and-caramel profile. That is the brand description, not our taste-test verdict. Matching flavor names also should not be stretched into a claim that the two formulas are identical ingredient for ingredient.

Four cans is not always the same amount of drink

A four-pack of 12 oz cans contains 48 fl oz in total. Four 8.4 oz Minis contain 33.6 fl oz. The larger four-pack therefore contains 14.4 fl oz more, even though both listings say four-pack. Compare delivered price and total volume together when judging an offer.

A mixed case is another separate purchase. Alani Nu's Potion Pack is described as six 12 oz Witch's Brew cans plus six 12 oz Ruby Apple cans. It is not twelve Witch's Brew cans. The brand markets Ruby Apple as the red-apple counterpart, so a shared apple theme does not make the flavors interchangeable.

Look for three details in a listing: flavor, volume per can and number of cans included. A photo with several cans is not enough if the quantity selector actually sells one can at a time. Read what one purchased quantity means before comparing prices.

Sold out on one website is not the end of the flavor

The official full-size and Mini pages presented a back-in-stock notification option when checked October 25. That is an online-store availability observation. Both pages continue to describe a seasonal product and provide a nearby-store route; neither establishes a nationwide final sale date.

For the final stretch before Halloween, check the exact format at the retailer you plan to use. A store may have Minis after full-size cans are gone, or vice versa. We have not verified a fixed national end date or a permanent return, and we would not infer either from a local empty shelf.

Discontinued Club has a separate 12 oz Witch's Brew four-pack. Its listing was active when checked October 25. The product page is the place to check current availability; this article is not a reservation or a guarantee of stock when you read it.

How to read this status

Seasonal return means a limited release is being marketed for the season. It is different from a permanent everyday listing, a sold-out batch at one retailer, or a confirmed permanent cancellation. We keep those claims separate.

Before choosing a pack

Check the actual can photos, printed dates, volume and package condition. For an older design, ask which run the seller holds rather than assuming it matches the newest brand image. Read the caffeine information and warnings on that specific package; Mini is a size name, not a caffeine-free claim.

Frequently asked

Alani Nu Witch's Brew questions

How much caffeine is in Witch's Brew Mini?

The official Mini page lists 100 mg per 8.4 oz can, compared with 200 mg per 12 oz full-size can.

Is Witch's Brew permanently discontinued?

The official 2026 pages market it as seasonal. An online sold-out status does not establish permanent discontinuation.

Does the Potion Pack contain only Witch's Brew?

No. Alani Nu describes that mixed twelve-pack as six Witch's Brew cans and six Ruby Apple cans, all 12 oz.
