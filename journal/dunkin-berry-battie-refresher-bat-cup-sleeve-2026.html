Dunkin Berry Battie is here. The bat cup sleeve arrives separately.

The drink and the collectible do not share a start date. That is the detail to check before planning your stop.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 14, 2026Updated October 16, 20263 minute read
The short answer

The drink starts October 14; the sleeve offer is October 16.

Dunkin announced the limited-time Berry Battie Daydream Refresher for its U.S. Halloween menu. Its bat cup sleeve giveaway is a separate promotion, subject to participating locations and available supplies.

What is the Berry Battie Daydream Refresher?

According to Dunkin's October 14 announcement, it combines berry-clementine and blueberry flavors with oatmilk, purple marshmallow cold foam and candy eyes. This is a prepared menu drink, not a canned soda.

We have not taste-tested it. Its appearance explains the Halloween styling, but a photograph cannot tell you how sweet it is or whether the flavor will be your favorite. This report is intended to make the ordering details clear, not to substitute promotional wording for a review.

How does the bat cup sleeve offer work?

The official photo above shows the sleeve itself, not just the drink: a dark cup wrap with purple trim and shiny purple bat wings. Dunkin's dedicated sleeve announcement shows the wings both extended and wrapped around the cup.

The published terms specify October 16: order a Berry Battie Daydream Refresher in any size for one free limited-edition bat sleeve, while supplies last at participating U.S. locations.

The practical next step is to ask that location whether it is participating and still has sleeves before paying. A drink appearing in the app does not, by itself, confirm accessory inventory. Do not assume another beverage qualifies, or that a sleeve is reserved merely because the drink can be ordered.

Treat the drink and the free item as separate decisions. Would you still want the drink if that store has run out of sleeves? If not, checking first avoids a disappointing purchase. If ordering several drinks for a group, ask how the one-sleeve-per-order limit applies before placing the order.

Why two dates matter

A launch headline can easily collapse several events into one. Here, the day the beverage joins the menu and the day the collectible is offered are distinct. Seeing the drink before the promotion does not mean a store is withholding an included accessory.

Save the official offer details, not just a cropped social image. A repost may show the product without the timing or participating-store restrictions. If you are reading this after the offer date, do not assume the promotion remains active.

Is this the same as the earlier green-apple release?

No. Our earlier Halloween menu guide covers the green-apple announcement. This page focuses on the separate Berry Battie release and its sleeve terms, so the shopping instructions do not get buried in an older seasonal roundup.

Dunkin also has a separately announced Doctor Doom collectible. Do not carry dates or purchase requirements from one promotion to another simply because they share the same brand.

How to read this status

Limited-time menu describes a seasonal offer, not a guaranteed permanent addition. A promotional accessory can run out independently of the beverage.

Before ordering

For dietary questions, check the complete current ingredient and allergen information for the full drink, including its topping. Do not infer suitability from oatmilk alone. Discontinued Club does not currently sell this drink or the sleeve.
