SKIPPY meets Chocottone. Here is what is actually new.

A chocolate-coated holiday loaf gets a familiar peanut-butter partner. But not every peanut-butter Chocottone listing is this new collaboration.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 9, 20264 minute read
The short answer

The SKIPPY collaboration is new. Peanut-butter Chocottone is not.

Bauducco announced its SKIPPY Peanut Butter Chocottone on October 6, with a U.S. grocery rollout beginning this month. The company had already introduced a Peanut Butter Chocottone in its 2025 range. Look for the SKIPPY name on the package to identify the newly announced version.

What is SKIPPY Peanut Butter Chocottone?

The new announcement describes a chocolate-coated sweet loaf filled with SKIPPY peanut butter. Bauducco is also introducing Pistachio Chocottone, a separate product rather than an additional filling in the SKIPPY loaf.

We have not sampled either one. The image is supplied by the manufacturer, so its presentation should be read as promotional artwork rather than evidence of how every slice will look.

Our interest is the product change: a recognizable second brand now appears on a format some shoppers may already know. That makes package identification more useful than a generic search for chocolate holiday bread.

Why do some peanut-butter listings look older?

Bauducco's November 18, 2025 release introduced Chocottone in Chocolate Chips, Peanut Butter and Hazelnut Cream versions. It described a ready-to-eat, airy loaf with chocolate chips and a fudge coating. The 2026 partnership therefore should not be reported as the first time the brand has made a peanut-butter loaf.

A Target record for Peanut Butter Chocottone uses the item number 94981440 and UPC 875754004785. It is a 16-ounce product, but its title does not name SKIPPY. That record is useful background; it is not independent confirmation that a store will send the new collaboration.

Matching weight and flavor are not enough to resolve a packaging transition. Ask the seller to confirm the brand names on the actual box if the partnership is the reason you want to try it. Do not assume that a familiar listing has been updated just because a new press release exists.

When and where is the new loaf launching?

The announced window is October 2026 at major U.S. food and grocery retailers. The release does not provide a complete chain-by-chain list, a universal first shelf day or a suggested national price.

We would start by searching the full Bauducco SKIPPY Chocottone name in the stores you already use. Open the item page, inspect its photo and select your local fulfillment location. A general brand landing page is not enough to establish that this specific loaf is stocked.

For online orders, compare delivered price and quantity. A single gift loaf and a multi-box shipment should not be treated as equivalent offers. Keep the listing screenshot or order description if you need a particular package for a gift.

What should buyers check on the box?

The older Target record lists wheat, peanut, milk, soy and egg, along with additional cross-contact warnings. We are not transferring that ingredient panel to the new SKIPPY item. Read the current package for its complete ingredients and allergen statement, especially before sharing it.

The announcement says more products from the partnership are planned but does not identify the next item or a release date. It also does not give a permanent-status commitment or a final sale date for this loaf.

That leaves a clear boundary for this report: the collaboration is real, but future flavors remain unnamed. A holiday presentation is not sufficient evidence to invent a discontinuation date. How to read this status

This is an announced U.S. launch. We have not independently verified the new SKIPPY package at every retailer, and older peanut-butter listings are not being presented as confirmed new stock.

Our buying takeaway

Confirm the SKIPPY branding, current label, package date and delivery timing before ordering. Discontinued Club does not currently sell this loaf or the separate Pistachio version.
