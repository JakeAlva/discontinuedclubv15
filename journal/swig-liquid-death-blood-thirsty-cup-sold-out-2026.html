Swig's Liquid Death cup sold out. The drink has one more day.

The souvenir and the sip are two different things. Here is what to ask for before making a trip.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 30, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Missed the cup? You may still be able to try the drink.

In its October 30 announcement, Swig says the collaboration cups sold out within 48 hours of the October 26 launch. Blood Thirsty continues in standard Swig cups at participating locations through October 31, while supplies last.

Blood Thirsty is not a can of flavored water

Swig's menu lists Blood Zero with raspberry puree, vanilla, fresh lime and vanilla cream. Separately, Liquid Death identifies Blood Zero as a raspberry-cream energy drink, not its plain or sparkling water.

That distinction is useful if you only recognize the tall-can branding. Buying the base from a retailer is not the same order as buying the prepared Swig drink. It also does not include the collaboration cup. Look for the full product name rather than treating every red can or branded cup as an interchangeable version.

We have not taste-tested the collaboration. The ingredient list suggests a fruit-and-cream direction, but it does not tell us how tart, sweet or creamy your finished order will taste. Nor should the nutrition information for the unopened base can be presented as nutrition for the drink after its additions.

Why the launch photo is not a stock check

The dated sellout notice is the source for the cup's status. During our review, the homepage still pictured the original offer. That is a useful reminder: promotional artwork can outlast the item it advertises.

Our reading is narrow. A souvenir selling out tells us about that souvenir, not the production future of every ingredient in the recipe. We would need a separate announcement before calling Blood Zero discontinued or describing the collaboration as a permanent menu addition.

For collectors, the photograph can help identify the design. It cannot establish resale value, rarity relative to another release or whether a secondhand listing includes an unused cup. Ask for actual-item photos and a clear condition description before paying extra for packaging.

Decide whether you want the flavor or the keepsake

If your goal is a first taste, ask the shop about the drink itself. If your goal is collecting the cup, ask that question separately. Being specific gives the staff a chance to answer the thing that matters to you instead of confirming only that a menu name still exists.

Use the official location finder and check with the participating shop before traveling. We have not checked any individual location's inventory. An article or social post is not a reservation.

Discontinued Club is not offering this drink or cup. Our Liquid Death x Pop-Tarts Carnage listing is a different collaboration, not a substitute listing for Blood Thirsty. Always compare the actual item rather than the brand name alone.

How to read this status

The stated promotional window ends October 31, 2026. Readers arriving later should treat this as a record of the Halloween offer, not a current availability promise. No cup restock is promised here.

Keep the offer in focus

Check the item, location and date together. A can, a prepared beverage and an empty collectible cup are different purchases, even when they share the same collaboration artwork.

Frequently asked

Swig x Liquid Death Blood Thirsty questions

Is Blood Thirsty the same as Blood Zero?

No. Blood Zero is the canned base; Swig adds other ingredients to make Blood Thirsty.

Can I buy the collaboration from Discontinued Club?

No. This is independent news coverage, not an offer from our inventory.

Does a cup sellout prove the drink is discontinued?

No. Packaging availability and a drink's production status need separate evidence.
