What is SUNNYD SpookyD? Halloween's mystery drink

The Halloween bottle is real. The flavor remains a mystery in the official sources we checked, and the two package formats deserve a closer look.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 20, 20263 minute read
The short answer

SpookyD is a limited-edition SUNNYD mystery drink.

Harvest Hill announced the Halloween release in August 2026, with distribution through Walmart while supplies last. This October guide covers how to find it and compare the bottles with the gallon jug.

What flavor is SUNNYD SpookyD?

The manufacturer's August 26 announcement deliberately leaves the flavor unidentified. That is the premise of SpookyD, rather than a missing word on the package. The brand invites people to make their own guesses.

We did not find a confirmed flavor name in the manufacturer announcement or the retailer pages linked below. A comment describing a particular candy or fruit is a tasting impression, not an official reveal. We have not sampled the drink ourselves.

Want to make a guessing game of it? Write down your own impression before reading anyone else's. Keep guesses separate from ingredient information, and leave the original label available for anyone who needs to check it. The mystery is about the advertised taste, not permission to conceal what someone is drinking.

Where can you buy SpookyD?

Walmart has separate pages for the 18-count pack of 6.75-fluid-ounce bottles and the one-gallon jug. Select your store before relying on pickup availability or the displayed price.

The retailer describes the small-bottle pack as shelf-stable and the gallon as refrigerated. Search both formats if only one appears initially, and follow the storage directions on the product you actually buy. One format being unavailable does not establish that the other has sold out.

The original announcement placed the start of the rollout in August. This is a seasonal shopping check ahead of Halloween, not a claim that the product launched today.

18 bottles or one gallon: what changes?

The small bottles are convenient for separate servings; the jug is a pour-and-share format. Our calculation from the labeled sizes: 18 bottles at 6.75 fluid ounces total 121.5 fluid ounces. A U.S. gallon is 128 fluid ounces, so the jug contains 6.5 fluid ounces more overall.

Compare price per fluid ounce if value is your priority: divide the pack price by 121.5, and the gallon price by 128. The number of containers is not the same as the amount of drink.

The listed nutrition serving sizes differ, too: Walmart shows 50 calories per small bottle and 60 calories per eight-fluid-ounce serving of the gallon. Those are not equal-volume servings. Check the label for your chosen format instead of transferring numbers from one package to the other.

How to read this status

The announced availability is limited and while supplies last. That does not establish October 31 as a hard last-sale date. Remaining stock may differ by store; an empty local shelf is not proof of a nationwide discontinuation.
