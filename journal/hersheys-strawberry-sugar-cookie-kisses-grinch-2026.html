Strawberry Sugar Cookie Kisses bring pink to Hershey's holiday lineup.

Look past the holiday character: the flavor name is the detail that separates this bag from the milk-chocolate options.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 30, 20263 minute read
The short answer

A seasonal flavor, not just another festive wrapper.

Hersheyland lists Cindy-Lou-Who Foils Strawberry Sugar Cookie Kisses in a 7.8 oz bag. The package describes strawberry-flavored creme and cookies. This is distinct from the milk-chocolate Kisses with Grinch foils also shown in the brand's holiday lineup.

Two official names can point to the same bag

Hersheyland's product page leads with Cindy-Lou-Who, while the official shop listing uses Grinch in its title. Both identify the same flavor and size; the product identifier ends in 467716.

That is a useful shortcut when two search results look different. Compare the flavor wording, net weight and product number before assuming one is a separate release. Character artwork is a good visual cue, but a broad holiday keyword is not a precise shopping list.

The holiday collection also includes Max-themed Hugs and Grinch milk-chocolate products. Those belong to the same seasonal theme, not necessarily the same flavor. A mixed photo showing several bags should not be read as a promise that one purchase contains them all.

Start with the exact listing, then check the total

The official Hershey store showed the single bag at $5.99 and marked it in stock when reviewed for this October 30 post. Treat that as a dated reference, not a guaranteed checkout price or a statement about your nearest retailer. Shipping and any order conditions can change what a small purchase costs.

If you only want to try the flavor, compare a single bag with the total cost of a multipack rather than buying extra solely because a page emphasizes a discount. For gifts, allow room in the delivery schedule and check the seller's packaging and shipping information. We have not placed an order to test fulfillment.

We do not have a verified first-on-shelf date for every chain, an official final sale date or a commitment that this flavor will return next year. Seasonal is the useful description; artificial countdowns would not help someone find the right candy.

A flavor guide, not a taste test

We have not tasted this release, so we are not scoring the strawberry intensity, sweetness or cookie texture. The package tells you what the manufacturer intends; a tasting review would require opening and trying the actual product.

For a baking idea, Hersheyland publishes a Cindy Lou Who Strawberry Blossom recipe that uses these candies as cookie toppers. Follow the original recipe for measurements and instructions rather than assuming this candy behaves exactly like a plain chocolate Kiss in every bake.

Discontinued Club is covering the release as candy news, not announcing incoming stock or taking preorders. Our journal follows seasonal products as well as discontinued favorites; an article appearing here does not mean the item has disappeared from normal retail.

How to read this status

Seasonal identifies the product's place in the holiday assortment. It does not establish scarcity, a production cutoff or a future resale premium. An out-of-stock message from one seller would not settle those questions either.

Keep the original label handy

Read the package for ingredients and allergen information, especially when sharing loose candies in a bowl. Do not substitute a different holiday variety based only on matching wrapper colors.
