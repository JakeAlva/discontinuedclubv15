Is Red Bull Arctic Berry back? Yes, with a catch.

The old flavor is part of a real comeback, but that does not make it a permanent standalone addition to the U.S. lineup.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 19, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Yes, in the 2026 Advent Calendar.

Red Bull identifies Arctic Berry as the calendar-exclusive Festive Edition. That is a limited return, not confirmation of a permanent U.S. single-can launch.

What returned, and when?

Red Bull North America's September 28 announcement introduced a U.S. Advent Calendar containing 24 assorted 8.4-fluid-ounce cans. Arctic Berry, originally the 2020 Winter Edition, is included as the Festive Edition. The brand describes its flavor as raspberry and says the calendar is available while supplies last.

The distinction matters if you remember the older can: a flavor can return under a different edition name and in a restricted package. For collecting purposes, the new front label should be recorded separately rather than called an untouched example of the old release.

Calendar availability versus individual cans

The announcement names retailers including Amazon, Target and Walmart. Those are distribution leads, not a live inventory check. The calendar is an assortment, not a case containing 24 Arctic Berry cans.

A single-can resale listing does not contradict a calendar-exclusive manufacturer release. It is a different selling format offered by that seller. Equally, the existence of individual listings does not prove a permanent national relaunch.

Before ordering, read the quantity carefully and compare the total delivered cost. Check that the photographs show the edition you want. Ask about package condition when collecting, and do not assume a listing is a sealed calendar just because its title mentions the holiday release.

Arctic Berry, Iced Vanilla Berry and Pomegranate are different

Red Bull's Iced Edition page describes Iced Vanilla Berry as blueberry and vanilla, and identifies it as a permanent product. That is not the raspberry-flavored Arctic Berry comeback covered here. A blue-toned can is not enough to identify the flavor.

The brand's U.S. Pomegranate FAQ also explicitly says Pomegranate is not in the 2026 Advent Calendar and points to Arctic Berry instead. Do not use an older calendar review as a packing list for this year's box.

For a useful search, include the flavor, edition, year and size together. That helps separate a current calendar item from an older collectible or a different drink with similar winter branding.

How to read this status

A limited comeback does not erase the history of an earlier discontinued edition. We distinguish the flavor, package and sales channel instead of applying one permanent yes-or-no label to every version.
