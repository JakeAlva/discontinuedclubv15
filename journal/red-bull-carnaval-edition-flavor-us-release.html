Red Bull Carnaval Edition: Is it coming to the U.S.?

A yellow can, colorful Carnaval lettering and a citrus flavor are drawing attention. Here is what the Brazilian listings tell us, and what they do not.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 20, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Brazilian listings exist. A U.S. release is not confirmed.

Red Bull Carnaval Edition is being reported for Brazil with the flavor Frutas Cítricas, meaning citrus fruits. As of October 20, 2026, we have not found an official U.S. launch announcement or a confirmed first-sale date.

What flavor is Red Bull Carnaval Edition?

GKPB's October 15 report names Frutas Cítricas and describes a limited Brazilian release in 250 ml cans, with regular and sugar-free versions. It does not identify a definitive blend of individual fruits.

That leaves an important gap between a flavor category and a tasting review. We have not sampled this edition, so we cannot tell you whether orange, lemon or another fruit dominates. A yellow label is not a recipe, and online tasting claims should not be treated as manufacturer confirmation.

Is it a real listing or just a social-media rumor?

There is more to check than a circulating image. Extra Mercado has a product page naming The Carnaval Edition, citrus flavor, zero sugar and a 250 ml can. The page reviewed for this article showed the item as unavailable.

Rappi also lists Carnaval Edition, with a sugar-free description, but displayed regional unavailability for Greater São Paulo. That is a location-specific result, not a statement about every shop in Brazil.

Our conclusion: retailer catalog entries support the product identity, but do not establish when customers can actually buy it. A product page can exist before stock arrives. Equally, an unavailable listing does not prove a drink has already launched, sold out or been discontinued.

When is Carnaval Edition coming out?

The reports are not equally specific. GKPB connects the edition with Carnaval 2027 and says it should arrive soon. Sporked's October 18 coverage describes an early-2027 release as probable and treats the news as a leak, rather than a finalized launch announcement.

Neither gives us a verified on-sale day. We are therefore not publishing a January release date, a countdown or a nationwide availability claim. The festival tie-in is useful context; it is not a substitute for a dated brand announcement or a retailer confirming stock.

Can you buy Red Bull Carnaval Edition in the U.S.?

We did not find Carnaval Edition in the official U.S. Editions information checked for this report. That is not proof it will never reach America. It means we cannot currently present it as an announced domestic release.

An importer offering a Brazilian can would be a separate development. If a listing appears, check the actual photos, country labeling, seller location and whether it is in hand or a preorder. Shipping a can to an American address is not the same thing as Red Bull launching that edition through U.S. stores.

Is Carnaval Edition the same as Sudachi Lime?

That has not been established. Red Bull's official Summer Edition description identifies lime, sudachi and a hint of pomelo. The Carnaval material reviewed here uses the broader citrus-fruit description without confirming a matching formula.

Similar colors and overlapping flavor families are not enough to call this a renamed Summer Edition. For now, treat them as separately labeled products with an unanswered formula comparison, not as proven duplicates or proven completely different recipes.

How to read this status

The status badge refers to the U.S. launch question. It does not dismiss the Brazilian retailer evidence. We separate three things: a product appearing in a catalog, a store having it available, and a manufacturer announcing its distribution.
