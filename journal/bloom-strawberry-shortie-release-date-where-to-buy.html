Bloom Strawberry Shortie is arriving before the rest of the treats.

The pink-and-purple can is more than a preview. Here is what is arriving now, what is still ahead and how to find the right pack.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 8, 20265 minute read
The short answer

Strawberry Shortie comes first. The other two follow in January.

Keurig Dr Pepper says Bloom Strawberry Shortie becomes available beginning in October 2026. Cotton Candy and Lemon Freezie are scheduled for January 2027. The three flavors share a treats-inspired theme, but they do not share one release window.

When does Bloom Strawberry Shortie come out?

The October 5 KDP announcement is the clearest source for the staggered schedule. Strawberry Shortie draws on strawberry and vanilla-cream flavors, while its later companions take inspiration from blue cotton candy and frozen lemonade.

The important detail is easy to lose when all three cans appear in one social post: a collection can be announced together and released in stages. Someone finding the first flavor this fall is not evidence that the other two have also reached regular retail distribution.

Likewise, a January plan should not be used to tell shoppers that every can in the collection is still months away. Read the flavor-specific date, not just the year in a beverage-industry headline.

Where can you look for it?

Target has a dedicated Strawberry Shortie listing for a single 12-fluid-ounce can. Its product identifier is TCIN 1013338580. That gives shoppers a precise item to search for or ask about, instead of relying on a search for pink Bloom. Set your location before treating a listing as a pickup option.

BJ's lists a Frosty Faves variety pack containing 18 cans: six Strawberry Shortie, six Shirley Temple and six Glacier Crush. That is a different assortment from the three-flavor announcement. It does not contain Cotton Candy or Lemon Freezie.

For a first try, a single can limits the commitment. A variety case makes more sense for someone who also wants the other included flavors. Comparing only the total can count can hide a poor match: six cans of the flavor you want are not the same purchase as eighteen of it.

Retail inventory changes by location and delivery method. A product page is useful evidence that an item is cataloged, but the final stock check belongs to the store fulfilling the order.

How much caffeine and sugar does it have?

Target's listing identifies 180 milligrams of caffeine, 0 grams of sugar and 10 calories per can. The caffeine is described as coming from coffee bean extract. Despite the frozen-treat illustration, this is a caffeinated energy drink, not a strawberry soda or a milkshake.

The BJ's ingredient list for Strawberry Shortie includes sucralose, taurine, L-theanine, apple cider vinegar and xylooligosaccharides. Its product warning says it is intended for adults, not children, and should not be consumed by people who are pregnant, nursing or sensitive to caffeine. Read the actual package for the complete current label.

We are reporting the product information, not endorsing the wider wellness claims used in energy-drink marketing. A dessert-inspired flavor name also tells you nothing by itself about allergens; anyone with a specific ingredient concern should check the can rather than infer a formula from the artwork.

Is it an ice cream collaboration or a limited edition?

The announced inspiration is an ice cream truck treat. That is not the same as a documented partnership with a named ice cream brand. We are not describing Strawberry Shortie as an officially licensed version of another company's dessert.

BJ's calls its Frosty Faves assortment limited edition. That label belongs to the assortment. It does not, on its own, establish an end date for standalone Strawberry Shortie cans. The announcement reviewed here does not provide a final sale date for the flavor.

That difference will matter later. If a seasonal case disappears, the useful follow-up question is whether the flavor remains available in another format. Treating every unavailable pack as a discontinued drink can create a false scarcity story.

Our GHOST A&W report covers a separate example of how a limited release and a permanent product plan can differ. The evidence has to follow the specific brand and item.

How to read this status

October rollout means there is a documented release window and supporting retail records. It is not a claim that we have visited every store, tested the drink or verified a universal shelf date.

What we would check before ordering

Match the flavor on the can, the package quantity and the fulfillment location. A listing for a mixed case should name every included flavor. Discontinued Club does not currently sell Strawberry Shortie or take advance orders for the later releases.

Frequently asked

Bloom flavor questions

Is Strawberry Shortie in the Frosty Faves pack?

Yes. The BJ's listing includes it with Shirley Temple and Glacier Crush, rather than the two later treats-themed flavors.

Is Strawberry Shortie confirmed permanent?

We have not found a permanent-status commitment or an end date in the announcement reviewed. A limited-edition variety pack does not settle the standalone flavor's future.

Have you tasted it?

Not yet. The flavor description here comes from the announcement and product listing, not a hands-on review.
