Dunkin has a Doctor Doom tin coming. Here is when to look.

The next collaboration reaches beyond the drink menu into collectible packaging. Here is what matters before planning a special trip.

By Discontinued Club ResearchChecked October 7, 20264 minute read
The short answer

The launch is November 4, not today.

Dunkin announced its Avengers: Doomsday collaboration on October 6. The limited-time U.S. rollout starts November 4 at participating restaurants, while supplies last.

What is the Doctor Doom tin?

Dunkin's official announcement lists the mask-inspired, 25-count MUNCHKINS tin at $14.99 with the treats included. Its promotional artwork states a three-per-guest limit and notes that price and participation may vary.

For a collector, the immediate question is whether the advertised price includes food or covers an empty container. Here, the launch offer includes the treats. A later resale listing may not, so read the description instead of assuming the original restaurant offer follows the tin everywhere.

We have not verified an online reservation process or a guaranteed allocation for any restaurant. Before traveling, ask the specific location about its participation and purchase rules. A national promotion does not tell us how many units an individual counter will receive.

What else is on the announced menu?

Two notable items are Doom's Orders Energy, combining Green Apple Dunkin' Zero with sparkling water and Purple Marshmallow Cold Foam, and the Doomsday Donut, which brings back Brownie Batter filling with themed icing and sprinkles. The announcement above contains the complete lineup.

We are highlighting the return of a filling, not claiming that an older donut design is returning unchanged. In seasonal food coverage, the recipe component, product name and decoration can have different histories. That distinction helps when a familiar favorite comes back wearing a new collaboration.

Before ordering a themed drink, check the actual customization and nutrition information offered by the restaurant. An energy-drink base, espresso drink and lemonade are not interchangeable simply because they belong to the same promotion.

Why do some of the ingredients sound familiar?

Dunkin's September 30 Halloween launch introduced Green Apple Dunkin' Zero and Purple Marshmallow Cold Foam. That earlier menu included Sour Apple Energy, pairing the apple base with sour lemonade and the purple topping.

Our Halloween menu report covers that separate release. Do not mistake an October photo of a purple-topped drink for evidence that the November merchandise is already on sale.

This also illustrates why we track a collaboration by its full product name and date. Seeing an ingredient return in another recipe does not prove the entire preceding menu has been extended. We have not established a common end date for the two promotions.

A practical checklist for launch week

Check the chosen restaurant rather than relying on a social post from another city. Ask about the tin specifically, since drink availability and merchandise inventory are different questions. Confirm the total price and whether there are additional local purchase limits.

For a secondhand collectible, request photographs of the actual container, including its lid, hinge and interior. Find out whether it is empty, used or still contains food. Promotional artwork identifies the design; it does not prove the condition of the item being sold.

There is no need to treat a preorder listing as proof of an official early release. A seller can advertise an expected item without having it in hand. Clear delivery terms and an accurate description matter more than an urgent caption.

How to read this status

Limited edition does not establish production numbers or future resale value. We will not describe this collectible as rare, sold out nationwide or certain to appreciate without evidence.
