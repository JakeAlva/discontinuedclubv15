Lay's holiday chips: five flavors, three release dates

Turkey and cheesy potatoes come first. Maple Bacon and two chocolate-covered chips follow on a different schedule.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 20, 20263 minute read
The short answer

The holiday lineup is rolling out in stages.

Lay's has announced five limited-time holiday offerings, but they do not all arrive together. The first two are scheduled for October 25, 2026; the chocolate-covered varieties follow in December.

When do the new Lay's holiday flavors come out?

The October 19 PepsiCo announcement sets out this U.S. schedule:

October 25: Cheddar Scalloped-Style Potatoes and Roasted Holiday Turkey begin their nationwide rollout, bringing cheesy-potato and turkey-and-gravy inspirations to the chip aisle.

November 8: Maple Bacon makes its U.S. debut.

December 1: Wavy Milk Chocolate and Wavy Dubai-Style Dark Chocolate launch at select retailers. The latter pairs dark chocolate with pistachios. Wider availability is planned for 2027.

That last distinction matters. An empty shelf in October would not tell you anything about the success or availability of a December release. Save the date for the specific bag you want, rather than treating the entire holiday collection as one launch.

The chocolate-covered chips are their own release

The official images show two visibly different groups: three seasoned potato-chip bags and two chocolate-covered Wavy varieties. The second photo below makes that difference much easier to see than a list of flavor names.

If you are planning a snack board or a tasting, think of those groups separately. A savory bag and a coated chip serve different roles, and a single mixed ranking can obscure what you actually want to buy. We have not sampled these products, so this is not a recommendation about which tastes best.

Where should you look for them?

Specific store chains are not named in the announcement. Search the full product name in your grocery or retailer app and select your location before checking pickup options. A national product page can exist before a nearby store receives any inventory.

For the coated varieties, confirm that the listing includes both Wavy and the chocolate type. For the seasoned chips, compare the words printed on the bag rather than relying on seasonal colors. The photographs here are useful identification references, not proof that a retailer currently has stock.

The first week of a rollout can be uneven. Check your normal grocery run before paying a reseller premium, particularly when the stated launch date has not arrived yet. A marketplace listing alone does not establish early nationwide availability.

How to read this status

This is an upcoming holiday release, not a discontinuation report. Limited-time does not give us one universal last-sale day, and we are not assigning one.

Keep the launch calendar separate from the shopping list

For holiday planning, note the dates now and verify stock nearer your event. Avoid building a menu around an unconfirmed delivery. Compare bag weights and the full delivered price if online sellers offer bundles.
