October 16, 2026 edition

Core Power Banana is coming back. Strawberry Banana is changing, too.

The banana-only bottles are no longer just a social-media claim. There is now an official announcement to read alongside the current lineup.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 16, 20264 minute read
The short answer

Yes, Banana has an official U.S. return announcement.

fairlife says Banana will return in January 2027 in 26g and 42g protein options, both permanent additions. It also says Strawberry 26g will replace Strawberry Banana. That is a future lineup change, not proof the existing flavor has already disappeared.

What did fairlife actually confirm?

fairlife's October 5 announcement identifies 14-ounce Banana bottles in two protein strengths, made with banana puree. It describes distribution expanding after the initial rollout, including online availability through its own shop. It does not promise every retailer will stock them on the same day.

That difference matters when planning a purchase. A launch month is a starting window, not a reservation or a store-level inventory count. There is no reason to interpret an empty shelf before the announced rollout as evidence that a comeback has failed.

Banana is not the same as Strawberry Banana

The current Core Power range still displays Strawberry Banana in the 26g line. Its dedicated product page names the combined flavor explicitly. Neither page should be shortened to Banana when identifying a bottle.

The fairlife shop also lists Strawberry Banana 26g and Strawberry 42g as separate products at this check. A reader can therefore encounter a real strawberry-only bottle without encountering the newly announced 26g version. The protein amount is part of the product identity, not a minor detail.

When searching, use the complete flavor and protein amount together. Saved retailer links, old reviews and short marketplace titles can otherwise send you to the wrong shake. A picture of fruit alone is a poor substitute for reading the front label.

What happens to Strawberry Banana?

The brand explicitly identifies Strawberry 26g as the replacement for Strawberry Banana. We are recording that as an announced replacement. We have not established a last production date, a final store-sale date or a nationwide sell-through deadline for the combined flavor.

This is the useful distinction for longtime buyers: a planned change can be real while the older product still has working product pages and remaining stock. Neither fact cancels the other. An old listing may persist after distribution changes, and an announcement may precede the physical change by months.

We also would not infer that a strawberry-only shake will taste exactly like the strawberry part of the old combination. We have not tried the new formulas. Removing one fruit from a name does not tell a reader how the full recipe, aroma or sweetness will compare.

What changed since our first report?

Our September 23 flavor-watch roundup examined reader-submitted reel frames. At that point, we had not verified an official announcement for the banana-only pair. This follow-up supplies the evidence that was missing, and the older page now links here.

We are not treating a later announcement as proof that every detail in an earlier mockup was authentic. A promotional concept, a final label and a production bottle are different records. The official bottle image shown here is the appropriate visual for this update.

The other drinks in that earlier roundup are separate claims. Confirming Banana does not automatically authenticate a Powerade bottle, a Topo Chico flavor or a vitaminwater formula shown alongside it. Each needs its own dated evidence.

What to check when bottles reach stores

Compare the same flavor, protein amount, bottle volume and pack count when looking at prices. A 12-pack total cannot be compared directly with a single-bottle price, and a 42g bottle is not simply another label for the 26g option. We are not assigning a launch price from today's prices for other flavors.

Use the current ingredient and allergen panel on the product you are buying rather than transferring a panel from an old listing. Our report covers availability and product identity; it is not a taste test, a nutritional recommendation or a claim that one protein level is right for everyone.

How to read this status

Confirmed return refers to the manufacturer's announced plan. Available now requires separate evidence from an actual selling location. We preserve that distinction so an early news story does not become a misleading stock promise.

Our store and this report are separate

Discontinued Club does not currently sell the announced Banana bottles. We are covering the return because it changes the status of a flavor readers have asked about, not because we have a preorder or a shipment to advertise.
