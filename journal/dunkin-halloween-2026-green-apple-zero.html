Dunkin's Halloween menu takes a green-apple turn.

Apple and marshmallow move into the spotlight. The useful distinction is not the color of the cup, but which drink you are actually ordering.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 1, 20263 minute read
The short answer

The Halloween drinks are officially on the menu.

Dunkin' announced a September 30 U.S. launch for Green Apple Dunkin' Zero and Purple Marshmallow Cold Foam. Both are limited-time offerings at participating restaurants, while supplies last.

What is Green Apple Dunkin' Zero?

The new menu announcement describes an apple-flavored sparkling energy drink. Midnight Apple adds blackberry flavor, Candy Kiss adds mango, and Orchard Twist adds peach. These are menu names, not fan-created secret-menu recipes.

The important word for ordering is the full name after the brand. A photo can suggest which color to expect, but it is a poor substitute for the ingredient description, especially if you are deciding between a fruit-forward drink and something with a creamy topping.

The purple foam is a separate part of the story

The seasonal marshmallow topping appears on several builds. One example is Sour Apple Energy: the apple base, sour lemonade and purple foam. It is not simply another name for the untopped Zero drink.

For readers following new energy drinks, that is the headline within the headline. Before comparing a cup with a bottled or canned zero-sugar product, identify the complete recipe. A base ingredient does not describe everything that ends up in the finished order.

Does every green-apple drink have zero sugar?

No. Dunkin's nutrition page links a guide updated September 21, 2026. It lists Green Apple Dunkin' Zero and Sour Apple Energy separately: the former has zero total sugars, while the latter has sugar. The guide also separates sizes rather than assigning one figure to the entire range.

Read the row for your finished drink, not just its base. Removing an ingredient or adding a topping can also make a standard menu entry an imperfect match. The current ordering information and restaurant staff are the right places to check a customized build; this article is not an individualized nutrition recommendation.

There is useful history here. The March 4 introduction of Dunkin' Zero established the lightly carbonated, zero-sugar energy platform with six fruit combinations. Its original lineup included Blackberry Tangerine, Tropical Mango and Juicy Peach. Green Apple is a later seasonal development, not the launch of a completely new beverage category at the chain.

Check your restaurant before making the trip

Use the official menu or app with your intended restaurant selected. We are not quoting a nationwide price: a delivery listing, a restaurant's own order screen and a customized drink can produce different totals.

This is an early-season guide, not a ranking. Without trying the drinks, we cannot tell you which tastes best or whether the apple reads more like fruit or candy. What we can do is keep the names, formula distinctions and available documentation straight so the eventual taste test starts with the right order.

How to read this status

A seasonal launch is not a discontinuation announcement. This page records an arrival; it does not predict a permanent slot on the menu or a future comeback.

We have not verified a final national selling date. A sold-out ingredient at one restaurant would not, on its own, establish that the entire program has ended.

Choose the drink first, then the customization

Start with the exact menu name instead of asking for the green Halloween drink. Confirm the size, additions and final price in the ordering screen. That makes it easier to compare the drink you receive with the product information you read.
