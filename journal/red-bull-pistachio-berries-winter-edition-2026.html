Pistachio & Berries is Red Bull's new Winter Edition.

A green can, a dessert-leaning flavor name and a seasonal release. Here is what is confirmed, plus the listing details worth checking.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 26, 20263 minute read
The short answer

This is an official seasonal product, not a mockup.

Red Bull's current U.S. product page identifies Pistachio & Berries as the new Winter Edition. It is marketed as seasonal, not as a permanently discontinued flavor. We checked the official page and our own single-can listing on October 26, 2026.

What Red Bull actually confirms

The official U.S. page describes a pistachio-led flavor with caramel notes and a berry finish. It lists 8.4 fl oz and 12 fl oz sizes. The smaller can is listed with 80 mg of caffeine and 26 g of sugar. Those are manufacturer figures, not results from a taste test or a laboratory check by us.

The U.S. page also says the flavoring is artificial and that the product does not contain nuts. That statement belongs to the product described on that page. For a purchase involving another market or a particular dietary concern, inspect the complete label on the actual can rather than relying on a flavor name or a marketplace dropdown.

Our listing is one regular 250 mL can

Discontinued Club's Pistachio & Berries listing is for one full, unopened 250 mL / 8.4 fl oz can. It contains sugar. It is not Sugarfree, a four-pack, an Arctic Berry can or a complete Advent Calendar.

The seller-confirmed printed best-by date is 06-02-27. We reproduce that text without converting an ambiguous day/month order into a calendar date. Minor dents, scuffs and scratches may be present, and mint display condition is not guaranteed. Ask for current label or condition details when a specific feature matters to your collection.

The listing was active when checked for this report. Availability on a product page is more useful than a quantity frozen inside an article: an order can change the remaining stock after publication.

Winter Edition is not the same listing as Festive Edition

Our separate Arctic Berry listing uses Festive Edition branding. The shared seasonal theme does not make those cans interchangeable. Compare the words printed under the Edition name before comparing prices, especially when search results mix several years and markets.

For a collector, the useful checklist is specific: exact flavor, packaging version, can volume, full or empty, condition and printed date. For a multipack, also check how many cans one selected quantity buys. Those details tell you more about the offer than a generic seasonal headline.

How to read this status

Seasonal is the supported status here. We have not verified a permanent place in the lineup or a single nationwide final sale date. A limited release should not automatically be described as discontinued.

Read the exact listing

An official brand announcement and a seller listing answer different questions. Use the brand for the release status, and the actual listing photos and label for the item you will receive.
