Pokemon Delta Reign prereleases start this weekend

Mega Rayquaza is on the box, but the date to circle depends on how you plan to play. Here is the difference between this weekend and the wider release.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 22, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Prereleases begin October 24. The full release is November 6.

Pokemon confirms that select Play! Pokemon Stores can offer Mega Evolution Delta Reign Build & Battle Boxes starting October 24, 2026. The expansion launches more broadly November 6. An early event is not a promise that every retailer has stock this weekend.

Three dates, three different ways to play

The official expansion announcement separates the rollout into prerelease events from October 24, a Pokemon TCG Live arrival on November 5, and the physical expansion launch on November 6. The digital date does not move the retail date forward.

For a weekend plan, look for an actual event listing with a start time and registration details. For a sealed-product purchase, confirm the seller's shipping or pickup date. A preorder confirmation and a reserved tournament seat are different things.

What is inside the Build & Battle Box?

Pokemon's prerelease guide lists four Delta Reign booster packs and a ready-to-play 40-card deck, including one of four foil promos: Masquerain, Magmortar, Enamorus or Kommo-o. The event format uses 40-card decks and four Prize cards.

That makes the pictured product different from a booster bundle. Do not compare two offers by the word "box" alone: check the exact product name and contents. The promotional image displays all four possible promos for reference, not a guaranteed four-card selection in every package.

Before paying for an event, ask what the entry fee includes and whether any additional packs depend on participation. Do not assume a particular store's event extras are standard contents of the sealed product.

Why Delta Reign is worth watching

The expansion brings Mega Rayquaza back to the TCG for the first time since XY Ancient Origins. It also introduces Legendary Stadiums: two Stadium cards work as a pair, with connected artwork. Pokemon says a booster containing one of these Stadiums includes both required halves.

Those are concrete reasons to follow the set. They are not evidence of guaranteed pulls, a short print run or future resale value. We are not publishing pack odds or an investment forecast, and have not opened this expansion for a review.

How to find a prerelease near you

Start with the official Play! Pokemon Event Locator, then contact the organizer to confirm the date, open seats, entry cost and any registration deadline. Ask whether you need to register before arriving and what you should bring.

A local sold-out event would describe that organizer's availability, not the production status of the entire expansion. Keep event capacity, sealed-box stock and manufacturer release plans separate when reading availability claims.

Discontinued Club does not currently list Delta Reign for sale. This is an independent release guide, not a preorder announcement. Our current card inventory is a separate selection of sets and formats.

How to read this status

As of October 22, this is an announced upcoming release. No discontinuation or final production date is established by the sources reviewed.

Match the product, not just the artwork

When comparing listings, check the expansion, language, sealed condition, pack count and exact box type. Similar artwork can appear on different products. Keep a copy of the listing description so you can check what was promised when the package arrives.
