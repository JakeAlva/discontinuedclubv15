Diet Coke Cherry is back. Here is the can to look for.

A returning favorite can be surprisingly hard to identify when several cherry colas share the same shelf. The name on the can matters.

By Discontinued Club ResearchChecked October 2, 20264 minute read
The short answer

Yes, this is an official U.S. return.

Coca-Cola announced a nationwide Diet Coke Cherry rollout beginning in February 2026. Its U.S. product page still lists the drink as of our October 2 check. This is a guide to that return, not an announcement of a new October launch.

What the U.S. announcement actually says

The February 2 announcement includes a U.S. and Canadian rollout in 12-ounce 12-packs and 20-ounce bottles. It specifies refreshed standard Diet Coke branding with cherry artwork, rather than the limited retro packaging seen in select markets.

That distinction helps explain why an older image is not a reliable shopping checklist. If your search is for the drink rather than a particular collectible can, matching the exact previous label could cause you to overlook a newer package. Keep packaging history separate from flavor availability.

Diet Coke Cherry is not Cherry Coke Zero

Coca-Cola's current U.S. cherry lineup lists five choices: Cherry Float, Cherry Float Zero Sugar, Cherry, Cherry Zero Sugar and Diet Coke Cherry. The Float drinks add a vanilla-flavor theme; Diet Coke Cherry is its own entry, not an alternative label for Cherry Zero Sugar.

For a clean comparison, write down the whole name rather than just cherry and sugar free. If a store offers only the other cola, that is a possible alternative, not confirmation that you found the same product. We are not claiming that one tastes better than another.

Readers hunting the newer float-inspired soda can use our separate Cherry Float availability guide. Keeping those questions on separate pages lets us track changes without treating every cherry product as one interchangeable flavor.

What the current label confirms

The U.S. Diet Coke product page uses the heading Diet Cherry Coke. Its 12-fluid-ounce entry lists zero calories, zero total sugars and 34 milligrams of caffeine. The ingredient list includes aspartame and acesulfame potassium. Sugar-free does not mean unsweetened or caffeine-free.

Use the package you receive for the final ingredient check, especially when buying an imported version or a different serving size. A flavor name alone is not a complete specification. This is a label summary, not a recommendation about whether a particular drink suits your dietary needs.

Why the British comeback has a different timeline

The British brand announcement is dated January 5, 2026. It describes a permanent return in Great Britain after a limited Tesco run in October 2025, with updated packaging replacing that retro presentation. Its listed formats include 330 mL multipacks, 500 mL bottles and 2-liter bottles.

Those are useful details if a social post shows a can unlike the one in a U.S. store. They are not grounds for moving a British launch date onto an American availability timeline. Nor should the word permanent from one market be silently applied to another.

Our answer for U.S. readers rests on the American announcement and current American listing. We have not verified a U.S. withdrawal notice or an end date. If one appears, that would be new evidence to weigh, not something an isolated out-of-stock message can settle.

How to read this status

The evidence supports treating this as a returning, currently listed U.S. drink. We are not assigning a historical nationwide discontinuation date: the sources linked here do not establish one.

Availability reporting is most useful when it separates the brand decision from the shopping experience. A retailer may not show the item for your selected store even while the manufacturer continues to promote it. Those observations can both be true.

A practical search starts with the full product name

Search both Diet Coke Cherry and Diet Cherry Coke, then inspect the photograph and item description together. Set the retailer to the store you actually plan to visit. Do not rely on a search-result price or a listing that defaults to another location.

If you are ordering through a delivery app, check whether substitutions are allowed. A different sugar-free cherry cola might satisfy a broad search but still miss the exact drink you wanted. Choosing the substitution setting before checkout is easier than trying to correct the order afterward.

Frequently asked

Diet Coke Cherry questions

Is Diet Coke Cherry discontinued in the U.S. now?

It is currently listed by the U.S. brand. The sources reviewed for this report do not support calling it discontinued as of October 2, 2026.

Is Diet Cherry Coke a different name for the same item?

The official product page uses Diet Cherry Coke, while the launch announcement uses Diet Coke Cherry. Check the product image and description when searching.

Does a British permanent return prove permanent U.S. availability?

No. A market-specific statement should not be extended to a different country without supporting evidence.
