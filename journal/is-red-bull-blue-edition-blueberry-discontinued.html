Is Red Bull Blue Edition Blueberry discontinued?

In the United States, the original blueberry edition is no longer part of Red Bull's current lineup. It is not gone everywhere, which is why apparently conflicting answers can both be true.

By Discontinued Club ResearchChecked September 11, 20269 minute read
The short answer

The original Red Bull Blue Edition Blueberry appears retired from the current U.S. lineup, but it is not globally discontinued.

Red Bull's current U.S. Editions page and its official flavor FAQ do not include the original Blue Edition Blueberry. Those same sources list the Editions Red Bull currently markets in the United States. However, Red Bull's Australian website still has an active product page for Blue Edition with the taste of blueberry. The most accurate answer is therefore regional, not universal.

What is the status of Blue Edition Blueberry in the United States?

As of September 11, 2026, Red Bull's official U.S. flavor FAQ presents a current list of Red Bull Editions. It includes flavors such as Amber Edition Strawberry Apricot, Iced Edition Iced Vanilla Berry, Sea Blue Edition Juneberry, Coconut Edition Coconut Berry, Yellow Edition Tropical, and Red Edition Watermelon. The original Blue Edition Blueberry is not on that list.

The full U.S. Editions product page tells the same story. It displays the current U.S. range and gives shopping paths for those products, but not for original Blue Edition Blueberry.

Red Bull does not need to publish a press release every time a flavor leaves a country. Because we did not find a formal U.S. discontinuation announcement, we avoid claiming a specific end date. The brand's own current catalog is still strong evidence that the original blueberry product is no longer an active U.S. Edition.

Why “discontinued” is not the whole global answer

Red Bull's product names and flavor colors are not identical in every country. Its Australian Blue Edition page remains active and explicitly describes the taste as blueberry. The page lists a 250-milliliter size and presents the drink as a current product.

That means a statement such as “Red Bull Blue Edition Blueberry is discontinued everywhere” would be incorrect. A better statement is: the original Blue Edition Blueberry is retired from the current U.S. lineup but remains available in at least some international markets.

Regional status also explains why imported cans can appear online after U.S. stock disappears. They may be current foreign-market products rather than leftover American inventory. Read the language, volume, nutrition panel, importer information, and country of manufacture before assuming two blue cans are the same release.

Blue Edition, Sea Blue Edition, and Iced Edition are not interchangeable

Red Bull now has several products whose names or flavors can be confused with the original blueberry can. In the current U.S. lineup, Sea Blue Edition is Juneberry. Iced Edition is described as blueberry combined with vanilla and icy eucalyptus notes. Coconut Edition also includes a berry component. None of those descriptions makes the drink the original Blue Edition Blueberry.

Color is not a dependable shortcut either. In some markets, the words “Blue Edition” can refer to a different berry flavor. The front label and stated flavor matter more than the can color alone. If you want the original, look for a can that explicitly identifies Blue Edition and Blueberry rather than Juneberry or Iced Vanilla Berry.

This naming drift is one reason search results are messy. A retailer may reuse an old image, merge reviews from multiple releases, or shorten the name to “Blue Red Bull.” Always inspect the actual product photo and description instead of relying only on a search-result title.

Why are original Blueberry cans still for sale?

A product can leave a national lineup without vanishing from commerce immediately. Distributors and stores may sell through remaining cases. Collectors may hold sealed cans for years. International versions may still be manufactured. Marketplace listings can therefore include U.S. old stock, imported current stock, empty display cans, or individual cans intended for collection.

That variety makes details important. Confirm the number of cans, size of each can, whether they are full or empty, visible condition, printed date, and shipping origin. A photograph of the exact item is more useful than a polished stock image when condition matters.

For older full cans, treat the purchase as a collectible first. Aluminum cans can leak or swell, and storage history is usually impossible to verify completely. A sealed package is not a promise that an aged beverage remains suitable to drink.

Why we call the status “Regional”

Our status labels are designed to answer the question without erasing uncertainty. “Confirmed Discontinued” requires a direct announcement or equivalent evidence. “Current” means the product remains in the relevant official range. “Regional” means the answer changes by market.

For original Blue Edition Blueberry, the evidence is unusually clear on that last point: it is absent from Red Bull's current U.S. list while still receiving an official Australian product page. If Red Bull returns the original blueberry edition to the United States, we will revise the headline answer and record the date.

Frequently asked

Red Bull Blue Edition Blueberry questions

Is Red Bull Blue Edition Blueberry discontinued in the U.S.?

It is no longer included in Red Bull's current U.S. Editions list. We classify the original flavor as retired from the current U.S. lineup, although the sources reviewed do not provide a formal end date.

Is Blue Edition Blueberry discontinued worldwide?

No. Red Bull's Australian website still lists Blue Edition with the taste of blueberry.

Is Iced Edition the same as the original Blue Edition?

No. The U.S. Iced Edition combines blueberry with vanilla and eucalyptus notes. It is a different flavor profile.

Is Sea Blue Edition blueberry?

No. Red Bull describes the current U.S. Sea Blue Edition as Juneberry.

Can I still buy the original flavor?

Old U.S. inventory and some international versions can still appear through specialty retailers and marketplaces. Verify the exact label, size, country, condition, and quantity.
