Is 7UP Miami Vice coming out?

A tropical-sounding name is getting soda fans talking. Here is what we could trace, what the name suggests, and why a convincing can image is not a launch announcement.

By Discontinued Club ResearchChecked September 25, 20264 minute read
The short answer

7UP Miami Vice remains an unconfirmed launch rumor.

As of September 25, 2026, we have not found an official U.S. announcement for 7UP Miami Vice. Public discussion exists, but we could not verify a launch date, final flavor description, retailer or authentic package image. Treat it as a product to watch, not a confirmed upcoming release.

Where is the 7UP Miami Vice rumor coming from?

One dated public trace is a September 25 discussion in Reddit's r/Soda community titled New 7up. Commenters discuss the Miami Vice name, possible flavors and whether they would be able to find it. That establishes public conversation around the idea, not manufacturer confirmation.

We could not establish the original source of the claim or authenticate the image behind that discussion. The thread should not be treated as a distributor notice, a retailer announcement or proof of a finalized launch plan. Reposts of the same claim would not, by themselves, provide independent confirmation.

What flavor would 7UP Miami Vice be?

The name has an existing drinks reference. Bacardi describes a Miami Vice cocktail as a combination of a pina colada and a strawberry daiquiri. Its recipe brings together pineapple, coconut and strawberry. That explains the flavor association; it does not establish what 7UP would put in a soda.

The same strawberry, pineapple and coconut idea appears in the Reddit discussion. Those are speculative flavor notes, not a verified 7UP formula. We have no ingredient panel, nutrition facts or tasting sample for Miami Vice. A cocktail-inspired name also cannot tell us whether a proposed soda contains juice, dairy, alcohol or any particular sweetener.

Is there a 7UP Miami Vice release date?

No U.S. release date has been verified in the sources we reviewed. We also could not substantiate a 2027 launch window. A year repeated in social conversation is still a claim until it can be connected to reliable product information.

The same uncertainty applies to regular versus zero sugar, cans versus bottles, package sizes and whether a release would be limited or permanent. We are leaving those details open instead of filling them in from another 7UP flavor. There is also no verified retailer exclusivity to report.

How is this different from 7UP Tropical?

The official U.S. 7UP site currently lists 7UP Tropical and Tropical Zero Sugar. Its description names mango and peach. That is an existing product listing, not confirmation of the strawberry-and-colada idea associated with Miami Vice.

Miami Vice was not listed on that page when we checked. An absent listing does not rule out future plans, but it means the page cannot serve as evidence of this release. Searching for a tropical 7UP product can lead to a real result without leading to the rumored product.

7UP has announced a different change this year

In its September 8 brand update, Keurig Dr Pepper describes a more lime-forward 7UP Lime Lemon formula and a refreshed visual identity. Updated packaging and a vertical logo are part of that announcement.

That is confirmed news, but it is separate from Miami Vice. A new-looking 7UP package does not automatically indicate a new named flavor, and the company's update does not announce a Miami Vice variant. The full product name matters more than a color scheme when identifying what is actually on the shelf.

How to read this status

Launch rumor / unconfirmed means there is something specific to investigate, but not enough verified evidence to tell readers the product is coming to U.S. stores. It does not mean we have established that the rumor is false.

This is a U.S.-focused report. A future announcement in another country would need its own market and timing checks before we described it as an American release.

Can you buy 7UP Miami Vice yet?

We have not verified a U.S. retail listing or an on-shelf sighting for this product, and Discontinued Club is not offering it for sale or preorder. There is no supported price or store list to publish at this point.

If a listing appears, look for a real package photograph, matching product information and a clearly identified seller. A concept render is not evidence that someone has physical stock. Do not read our illustration as a preview of a can you can order.
