Is Pepsi Blue back? A 2021 bottle is not a new U.S. launch.

The bright-blue berry cola still turns up for sale. The date on the release matters more than the date on the listing.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 15, 20264 minute read
The short answer

No new U.S. return is confirmed in the sources we checked.

Pepsi Blue is a discontinued U.S. release with a documented limited comeback in 2021. A bottle offered for resale today does not establish that Pepsi has started making it again.

When did Pepsi Blue come out, and when did it return?

2002: Pepsi Blue made its original debut. 2004: it left the U.S. market. Those dates appear in USA TODAY's 2021 reporting, republished by the Chicago Sun-Times.

2021: Pepsi announced a limited national comeback, with availability by May 3 while supplies lasted. That is the return behind many search results that still say Pepsi Blue is coming back. Check the article's year before treating that headline as fresh news.

A release can have more than one life. The original early-2000s run and the later nostalgia release belong on the same timeline, but a bottle from one should not be presented as a bottle from the other.

Is Pepsi Blue discontinued in the United States now?

BevNET's brand directory marks Pepsi Blue discontinued. It also does not appear among the products on the current Pepsi U.S. site at our October 15 check. Combined with the limited-release history, those sources support treating it as a discontinued U.S. release rather than an ordinary current supermarket flavor.

There are limits to that conclusion. The directory does not establish the last day a bottling line produced it, and a brand website is not a complete inventory of every warehouse. We are not assigning a precise end date to the 2021 run or claiming there are no bottles left to buy.

We also have not established an official reason for its withdrawal. Claims that a drink disappeared because of poor sales, an ingredient ban or a secret replacement need their own evidence. The release history alone does not prove any of those explanations.

Was there really a 16.9-ounce Pepsi Blue bottle?

Yes. Elite Daily's April 25, 2021 report, citing information supplied by Pepsi, identifies 20-ounce bottles and six- and eight-packs of 16.9-ounce bottles. A smaller bottle is therefore consistent with the documented U.S. release; the size alone does not make it an import.

The same report describes the product as berry-flavored cola and distinguishes that from fans' guesses about particular berries. We have not taste-tested the bottle pictured here and are not assigning it a blueberry, raspberry or candy comparison of our own.

Where can collectors find Pepsi Blue?

Resale listings, including eBay, can offer older bottles. Our Pepsi Blue listing identifies a single unopened 16.9-fluid-ounce bottle from 2021. That is an addition to our store, not an announcement from Pepsi. See the product page for current stock and price.

Before buying an older bottle, check the release year, whether it is full or empty, the seal, label condition and the exact quantity included. Ask for photographs of any date code if it matters to your collection. A recent listing date says when an item went on sale, not when its contents were made.

We sell the pictured bottle as an older collectible, not as fresh drinking stock. We have not verified its expiration date, and an intact seal is not a promise of freshness or suitability for drinking.

For a genuine comeback, look for a newly dated announcement specifying the U.S. market and distribution window. A reposted 2021 article, a collector listing or a bottle sold in another country would not answer that question on its own.
