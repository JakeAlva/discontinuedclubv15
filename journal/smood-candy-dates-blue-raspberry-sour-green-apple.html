SMOOD Candy Dates add Blue Raspberry and Sour Green Apple.

A familiar candy-aisle flavor is moving onto a very different kind of center. The new blue bag contains dates, not blue raspberry gummies.

By Discontinued Club ResearchChecked October 8, 20265 minute read
The short answer

Two new flavors, with a confirmed Blue Raspberry retail listing.

SMOOD SWEETS announced Blue Raspberry and Sour Green Apple Candy Dates on October 8, 2026. The company says Blue Raspberry is rolling out to approximately 1,900 Target stores. That does not mean both new flavors are stocked at every Target.

What did SMOOD announce?

In its brand-issued release on Nombase, SMOOD describes Sour Green Apple as an addition for fans of sour candy and Blue Raspberry as a nostalgic candy flavor. The announcement also names Fresh Thyme, Better Health Market, Good Earth Market and TJX banners in its retail expansion, with Hy-Vee planned toward the end of the year.

Those are not all identical availability claims. A chain joining the brand's distribution does not tell us that every location carries every flavor. The specific Target statement concerns Blue Raspberry; we would want a separate listing before directing a shopper there for Sour Green Apple.

This report covers the U.S. expansion announced today. It is not a claim that today was the first time anyone could have encountered a sample or an early retail listing.

What are Candy Dates, exactly?

SMOOD's own explanation starts with whole pitted dates and adds a crunchy flavored shell. The fruit supplies the center, so this is not a conventional gummy simply shaped or flavored to resemble a date.

That makes the product worth explaining before calling it a substitute for a favorite candy. A familiar flavor name can set an expectation of a uniform gummy or a hard candy. Here, the core is a piece of fruit. We have not tasted the new flavors, so we are not claiming that either duplicates a particular candy brand.

The company notes that pieces naturally vary in shape and size. An irregular-looking piece is therefore consistent with the whole-fruit format, rather than evidence that every item should look like the polished illustration on the bag.

For someone deciding whether to try it, the useful question is whether a fruit center sounds appealing alongside the chosen coating. Blue Raspberry names the flavor direction; it does not mean the fruit inside is a raspberry.

Where can you find Blue Raspberry Candy Dates?

Target's Blue Raspberry page identifies a 3-ounce bag, with TCIN 1010230365 and UPC 850053039775. Those identifiers help distinguish it from the brand's other flavors. The page is a useful place to check pickup or shipping after selecting your location.

Fresh Thyme also has a 3-ounce Blue Raspberry product record matching that UPC. The page reviewed is associated with its Dublin, Ohio store context. That is evidence of a retailer listing, not a nationwide stock count or a promise of inventory at a different branch.

Our suggestion is to search the exact name before making a special trip, then verify the store and fulfillment option. Retail search tools sometimes surface related flavors when the one requested is unavailable. A result headed Candy Dates is not enough if you specifically want the blue bag.

We are not publishing a single national price. Promotions, local pricing and online fulfillment charges can make a screenshot of one store a poor guide to the cost at another.

No added sugar is not the same as sugar-free

Target's label information lists 13 grams of total sugar and 0 grams of added sugar per 30-gram serving, along with 80 calories and 2 grams of fiber. That is a clear reason not to shorten the package claim to sugar-free. The figures are for a serving, not the entire pouch.

The ingredient list includes dates and maltitol. It also carries a warning about possible pits or pit fragments and notes a facility that processes almonds, peanuts, pecans, milk and soy. Check the bag for the latest complete ingredient and allergy information rather than relying on the front-of-pack claims alone.

We have not verified a matching nutrition panel for Sour Green Apple, so we are not copying the Blue Raspberry numbers over to it. Sharing a brand and a product format does not make two flavors' labels interchangeable.

How to read this status

New flavor coverage is not a discontinuation report. Nothing in this announcement establishes that an older Candy Dates flavor is being removed. A growing retailer list and a changed assortment should be checked separately.

A different kind of candy-aisle experiment

This launch is interesting because it pairs recognizable candy flavors with a whole-fruit format. Whether that combination works for your taste is still a personal call, not a conclusion we can draw from a launch release. Discontinued Club does not currently sell these products.

Frequently asked

Candy Dates questions

Are these gummies?

No. The brand describes whole pitted dates with a candy coating, rather than a conventional gummy center.

Is Sour Green Apple confirmed at Target?

The reviewed announcement specifically connects Blue Raspberry with the Target rollout. It does not establish the same distribution for Sour Green Apple.

Is this a taste review?

No. We checked the announcement and product records, but have not tasted the two new flavors.
