Hint turns key lime pie and three more desserts into water.

Dessert names, no bubbles. The new collection takes a different approach from the dessert-flavored sodas filling the drinks aisle.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 15, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Treat Yourself is a new limited-edition still-water collection.

Hint announced the collection on October 14. These are flavored waters, not sodas, shakes or liquid desserts. The official product listing is live.

Which flavors are in the Hint dessert-water pack?

The launch announcement names Key Lime Pie, Strawberry Cupcake, Pineapple Whip and Berry Cobbler. Hint describes lime with vanilla, strawberry with a cake-inspired note, creamy pineapple flavor, and blueberry-blackberry with cinnamon, respectively. Those are the manufacturer's descriptions, not our tasting notes.

The useful distinction is the drink underneath those names. A cupcake illustration might suggest a sweet, substantial beverage, but this collection belongs in the flavored-water category. Someone seeking a cream soda should not order it expecting that texture or sweetness.

Does dessert-flavored water contain sugar or sweeteners?

The official listing identifies purified water and natural flavors as the ingredients and states that it contains no sugar or sweeteners. It lists a 16-fluid-ounce serving and zero calories. The dessert imagery describes the flavor inspiration; it is not an ingredient list.

For a dietary restriction, use the current bottle label and manufacturer information rather than drawing conclusions from a flavor name. This article is a product-news report, not an endorsement of a nutritional benefit.

Where to buy it, and why the prices may differ

Hint names select retailers, including Walmart, plus Amazon and its own website, with an announced suggested price of $18.99 per 12-pack. That is a launch reference price, not a promise that every seller charges the same amount. Source: Hint's release.

On October 15, the brand's checkout options instead showed $22.99 for a one-time order and $18.99 with a subscription. Check the selected purchase frequency and delivered total before comparing offers. We have not placed an order or verified stock at individual stores.

A lower recurring-order price is not automatically the better choice for a first taste. Consider whether you want repeat deliveries before selecting a subscription simply to match a price mentioned in a headline.

A caution when reading the product reviews

At our check, the Treat Yourself page also displayed reviews referring to other assortments, including Summer Classics and ice-pop flavors. Those comments are not reliable evidence of how this particular new collection tastes. We cannot determine from the page why those reviews appear together.

Before using a star rating to choose a new release, read the review text for the actual flavor and package being discussed. A long-running variety-pack listing can be useful for learning about ordering experiences without every old comment applying to its newest assortment. We have therefore not presented the aggregate rating as a score for these four dessert flavors.

How to read this status

Limited edition does not supply a precise sellout date. We would need a further announcement to report that this collection is permanent, discontinued or returning for another season.

The detail worth checking first

Confirm that the listing is the Treat Yourself assortment, not a different Hint variety pack. Discontinued Club does not currently sell this collection; the links above lead to the reporting sources and manufacturer.
