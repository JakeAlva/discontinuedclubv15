New snack formatSnack aisle watch

TRUBAR steps beyond bars with dessert-inspired rice cakes.

A familiar name on a different kind of snack bag. Here is how to identify the new release.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 28, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Drizzle is a new format, not a renamed protein bar.

TRUBAR announced Drizzle on October 27, 2026: mini rice cakes in birthday-cake, s'mores and cinnamon-roll flavors, launching at Walmart. The brand describes it as its first major expansion beyond protein bars.

Three flavors, with very specific names

The official release names Hey Shorty, It's Your Birthday Cake; S'more to Love; and Cinnamon Roll With It. It lists 4 oz bags and a ten-count variety pack containing individual 1 oz bags.

Use the full product phrase "TRUBAR Drizzle mini rice cakes" when searching. A result that only says TRUBAR might lead to a bar, a different package or an assortment that does not contain the flavor you wanted. The word Drizzle and the rice-cake description do more identification work than the brand logo alone.

Compare ounces before comparing the price tag

The announced starting prices are approximately $4.74 for the larger bag and $11.99 for the variety pack. Calculated from those figures, both formats land close to $1.20 per ounce. Those are reference prices from the announcement, not checkout quotes from a local store.

For a first purchase, decide whether you want to try a particular flavor or have separately wrapped portions. Count the total ounces across a multipack before treating a higher package price as worse value. Conversely, a larger outer box does not automatically mean a lower unit price. Shipping, pickup minimums and retailer promotions can change the comparison.

A search result with a product photograph can still be the wrong pack size. Open the item details and compare the title, net weight and number of bags together. If those fields disagree, stop and clarify rather than assuming the biggest number on the page is the quantity you will receive.

What we would check before making a trip

Set your store location first, then look for an actual pickup or delivery option for the exact item. A nationwide retailer name in an announcement is not a promise that every branch received the product simultaneously. A delivery listing may also be offered by a different seller from the one operating your local store.

We have not taste-tested these rice cakes, so this is not a texture ranking or a claim that one flavor is best. Treat dessert names as flavor descriptions, not evidence that the snack contains the same ingredients as the dessert itself. Read the current package for ingredients and any dietary requirements.

For our archive, the important distinction is addition versus replacement. A brand entering another snack category does not by itself establish that its earlier products are leaving. We would look for a separate manufacturer statement before attaching a discontinued label to an existing bar.

How to read this status

This is a new-product report, not a store-stock alert or a discontinuation notice. We have not independently checked a local Walmart shelf.

Match the bag you actually want

Keep the flavor name, package size and seller together when comparing offers. Do not assume an online substitution has the same formula or portion count. The final package label should settle ingredient questions, not a shortened search-result title.
