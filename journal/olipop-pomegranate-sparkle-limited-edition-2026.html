OLIPOP Pomegranate Sparkle is real. Is it here to stay?

The new holiday can has a name, an official listing and a limited run. Here is what the brand confirms, and what needs a label check.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 14, 20263 minute read
The short answer

It is a limited-edition holiday flavor, not a confirmed permanent addition.

OLIPOP identifies Pomegranate Sparkle as a seasonal blend of pomegranate, quince and passion fruit. The brand says it is offered online and through U.S. retailers for a limited time. We found no specific nationwide end date or promise that it will become permanent.

What does Pomegranate Sparkle taste like?

The official flavor guide describes a tart-and-sweet fruit profile built around pomegranate, quince and passion fruit. It is a holiday soda, but not a vanilla-cola or baking-spice flavor.

That is the intended profile, not our tasting verdict. We have not opened a can for this report, so we are not rating its sweetness, aftertaste or resemblance to another brand. The clearest reason to try it is an interest in that particular fruit combination, not an assumption that a festive can guarantees a favorite.

Is it discontinued already, or just limited edition?

Both the product listing and the brand guide identify it as limited. The listing also displayed an out-of-stock message during our check. That is evidence about the brand's online offer, not proof that every retail can is gone.

There are three different questions here: whether a flavor exists, whether a particular seller has stock, and whether production or distribution has ended. A shopping page can answer the first two without settling the third.

For now, the useful description is a confirmed limited-edition release. We are not converting a stock message into a discontinuation headline or promising another batch.

Why we are not giving one definitive calorie figure

At review, the nutrition table on the product page listed 30 calories and 2 grams of total sugar per can. The separate flavor guide listed 35 calories and 4 grams of total sugar. Both listed 6 grams of fiber.

We cannot determine from those pages alone whether this reflects an outdated entry, a packaging revision or another publishing error. It would be misleading to select one set of numbers and call the difference resolved.

Use the nutrition and ingredient panels on the actual can you are buying. If a precise amount matters to you, ask OLIPOP to confirm the current formula against that package. This article does not make a digestive-health claim or evaluate the benefits of the ingredients.

Where should you look for the right can?

Start with the official product page and its Find in Store option. Confirm the exact flavor with a local retailer before traveling. A store appearing in a brand locator is a lead, not our independent verification of a can on its shelf.

Check the selected pack size when comparing prices. One can, a case and a variety pack are not interchangeable offers; shipping can also change which listing is the better value.

Keep the shopping question separate from the collecting question. A seasonal package may interest collectors, but that does not make a newly released soda scarce enough to justify whatever a reseller asks.

How to read this status

Limited edition describes the announced release type. The available sources do not establish a final production date, a guaranteed return or a permanent place in the lineup.

Our takeaway

Look for the exact package, compare the delivered price and check the can label. Discontinued Club does not currently sell Pomegranate Sparkle, and this report is not a stock guarantee.
