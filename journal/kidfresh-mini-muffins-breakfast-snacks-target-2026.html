Kidfresh mini muffins are heading for the freezer aisle.

The brand is moving further into breakfast and snacks with four products announced today. The mini muffins are not a bakery-aisle release.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 15, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Four new frozen products, with an October Target rollout.

Kidfresh announced the lineup on October 15, 2026. Blueberry and Chocolate Chip Mini Muffins join Egg Bites & Potato Tots and Mac & Cheese Bites in the launch.

What did Kidfresh announce?

In its October 15 announcement, Kidfresh names two muffin flavors, Blueberry and Chocolate Chip, alongside Egg Bites & Potato Tots Easy Combo and Mac & Cheese Bites. It gives October 2026 at Target as the launch window.

The savory additions serve different occasions: the combo pairs eggs with tots, while the macaroni product uses a bite-sized format. The two muffin flavors make the news relevant to shoppers looking for breakfast or snacks, not just another frozen dinner.

These are new-product announcements, not evidence that an existing Kidfresh meal is going away. We have not found a replacement or discontinuation claim in the announcement, and we would not infer one simply because a brand is adding more choices.

Where will you find the new Kidfresh muffins?

The rollout is for the freezer aisle. Target's Blueberry Mini Muffins page already identifies a 6.2-ounce package containing eight mini muffins. The product photography says to keep the package frozen.

That changes where to look: a fruit-flavored mini muffin may sound like a shelf-stable lunchbox snack, but this listing belongs with frozen products. Use the storage and preparation directions on the package rather than treating it like a bakery muffin.

Select your location on the retailer page to check pickup or delivery. A national product listing can establish that a retailer has created an item record, but it does not establish that every branch has received its shipment. We have not checked individual stores or verified a second retailer for this launch.

What does the Blueberry Mini Muffins label say?

At our check, the Target label panel lists a 44-gram serving and four servings per container. Each serving has 140 calories, 5 grams of protein, 9 grams of total sugar and 7 grams of added sugar. Those figures are per serving, not per individual mini muffin.

The ingredient list includes butternut squash, wheat flours, banana and blueberries. It also includes white cane sugar and agave syrup. The presence of vegetables does not mean the product is sugar-free or establish that it is the best choice for a particular child.

Use the actual package for current ingredients, allergens and serving information. We are reporting a product label, not making a nutritional recommendation or promising a health benefit. We have not tasted these muffins, so there is no texture or flavor verdict here.

Are the new muffins limited edition?

The launch release does not set a limited-edition window or final sale date. That is different from an explicit promise that a product will remain in stores permanently.

For shoppers, the useful next evidence is local availability and the package itself. For anyone following product news, a later expansion to more retailers would be a separate development to verify, not something to assume from this announcement.

We are broadening the journal to follow changes across the grocery aisle. The same distinction applies here as it does to a new soda: an announced rollout, a live retailer page and confirmed local stock are three different pieces of information.
