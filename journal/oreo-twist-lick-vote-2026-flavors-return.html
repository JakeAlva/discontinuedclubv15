OREO flavor vote: the three contenders and the result

Banana Pudding, Deep Fried and Chicken & Waffles competed for another turn on the shelf. Banana Pudding won; a permanent place in the lineup was never the promise.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished September 25, 2026Updated October 20, 20264 minute read
The short answer

Voting is closed. Banana Pudding won.

Update, October 20: OREO has confirmed Banana Pudding as the Twist Lick Vote winner, with a return to stores in 2027. Voting closed October 12, 2026. This guide preserves the three-flavor campaign history; our linked follow-up covers the comeback.

Which three OREO flavors were in the vote?

The brand's August 13 announcement describes Banana Pudding as banana and vanilla pudding creme between vanilla wafers. Deep Fried combines fried-dough-flavored cookies with dark chocolate and fried-dough-flavored creme. Chicken & Waffles uses waffle-textured cookies and a sweet-savory creme profile inspired by fried chicken and maple syrup.

These are three different cookie concepts, not three wrapper designs for the original flavor. The names explain the inspiration; they should not substitute for reading a package's ingredient and allergen information.

When did voting close?

OREO's official Twist Lick Vote page now shows the result. The published deadline was October 12, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Eastern. A voting invitation printed on an original package does not reopen that period, and a later social-media poll does not change the official outcome.

The associated sweepstakes rules remain a historical reference, not an invitation to enter now. The cookie vote and prize promotion were not a reservation system. Participating did not place a product order or guarantee that a nearby store would stock the winning flavor.

What is actually coming back in 2027?

OREO originally scheduled the winning-flavor announcement for October 13. The official page now identifies Banana Pudding. The original promise was another limited-time appearance in 2027, not permanent placement in the core range. An exact 2027 shelf date is still missing from the reviewed announcement and result page.

Read our Banana Pudding OREO comeback report for the current return details. This is not a promise that all three contenders will come back. A favorite flavor and an announced future product are different things; the posted result resolves only the latter for the winner.

Are the current flavors already discontinued?

The announcement set the U.S. retail rollout for August 24, 2026, following an August 17 presale. It described a limited run while supplies last. We have not verified that the current run has ended, and this report is not a nationwide inventory check.

If one flavor is missing from a store, that alone cannot tell you whether it sold through, never arrived there or has reached the end of its run. The absence is useful for planning your shopping, but it is not enough to declare a national discontinuation.

A comeback story before the disappearance

Most questions about discontinued products arrive after a favorite is gone. This campaign gave readers a chance to follow the decision about a second run while the original flavors were still a recent release. The result is now something we can verify rather than a prediction based on social-media enthusiasm.

The next useful reporting checkpoint is a specific return schedule. Until that is published, an announced year should not be converted into a precise shopping date. We have retained this article as the campaign guide and linked the follow-up so older search results do not leave readers with an outdated invitation to vote.

How to read this status

Vote closed / winner confirmed describes the campaign, not a finding that all three products have disappeared from U.S. stores. We are not labeling a flavor discontinued just because it is difficult to find at one retailer.

A planned comeback also does not guarantee continuous availability between the current run and the next one. The distinction to follow is current stock, the voting result and the eventual return schedule.

What to check before buying a pack

Search the complete flavor name and compare the actual front label. Do not assume a marketplace photo proves the seller has fresh stock, or that an unusually high resale price means the flavor has been permanently retired. Check local retail options first.

We have not conducted a taste test, so there is no best-flavor ranking here. The descriptions below are the manufacturer's descriptions, not our tasting notes. This story covers the announcement independently of anything available in our shop.
