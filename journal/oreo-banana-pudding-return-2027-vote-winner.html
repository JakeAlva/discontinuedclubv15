Banana Pudding OREO won. It is coming back in 2027.

The flavor-vote result is official. The comeback has a year, but not yet a specific shelf date.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 20, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Yes, Banana Pudding OREO is returning.

OREO's official campaign page names Banana Pudding as the winner and says it will return to stores in 2027. That confirms the comeback, not a nationwide restock today.

Which OREO flavor won the 2026 vote?

OREO's Twist Lick Vote page now presents Banana Pudding as the winner. The result resolves the question from our original three-flavor campaign guide, which we have also updated.

The result page does not provide a vote total or winning margin. There is no need to turn the announcement into a claim about a landslide or a record-breaking contest. The useful news for shoppers is simpler: this particular flavor has a confirmed second run ahead.

When will Banana Pudding OREO return?

The confirmed window is 2027. The original August 13 campaign announcement promised the winning cookie a limited-time return. Neither that announcement nor the result page reviewed for this article supplies an exact 2027 date.

Do not read the year as a January launch, a preorder opening or a promise that every grocery chain will carry it. Those are separate details that need a dated announcement or retailer listing of their own.

For readers deciding whether to buy a remaining pack now or wait, the trade-off is timing rather than certainty about the recipe. Waiting means accepting an unspecified release window. Buying now means checking the actual current stock and its package date, rather than assuming a future comeback makes every online listing fresh.

What is Banana Pudding OREO?

The 2026 cookie combines banana and vanilla-pudding flavored creme with vanilla wafers, according to OREO. It is a dessert-inspired cookie, not the standard chocolate OREO with a yellow wrapper.

The official package photo is included so readers can identify the product accurately. Its voting invitation belongs to the original promotion, not a new voting period. It also should not be mistaken for a revealed redesign of the returning package.

We have not taste-tested it. Flavor names and manufacturer descriptions can explain the concept, but they cannot tell you whether the banana note will match your favorite homemade pudding. That is a separate question from whether the return is real.

How to read this status

A confirmed comeback is not the same as uninterrupted availability. This report does not establish that every store has sold out of the original run, and it does not label all remaining packs discontinued inventory.

What happens next?

The next useful details are a specific release date, package sizes and participating retailers. Until those appear, treat purported launch calendars as unconfirmed unless they point back to OREO or an identifiable retail listing.

For the full history of the competition, including the other contenders and the closed voting period, use our original campaign guide. This follow-up focuses on the winner and the comeback, so readers do not have to work through old voting instructions to find the answer.
