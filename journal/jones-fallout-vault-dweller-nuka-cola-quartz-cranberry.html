Fallout gets two new Nuka-Cola sodas

Quartz and Cranberry join Quantum in a collector-focused variety pack. Here is how to identify the new release and check for it without confusing an announcement with local stock.

By Discontinued Club ResearchChecked September 25, 20264 minute read
The short answer

A real release, with a retailer listing to match.

Jones Soda has announced the Fallout Vault-Dweller Variety Pack, and Costco Business Delivery lists the exact assortment under item 2067687. This is a confirmed product, not a social-media mockup. A listing does not establish availability at every warehouse or delivery address.

What is in the Fallout Vault-Dweller pack?

The Costco Business Delivery product record specifies 12 bottles, each 12 fl oz (355 mL): four Nuka-Cola Quartz, four Nuka-Cola Cranberry and four Nuka-Cola Quantum. It identifies the brand as Jones Soda and describes the drinks as made with cane sugar.

The product photo labels Quartz as vanilla cream soda, Cranberry as cranberry-flavored soda and Quantum as berry lemonade soda. Those are label descriptions, not our tasting notes. Search for the full pack name to distinguish this mixed case from individual bottles or older assortments.

Quartz and Cranberry are the new arrivals

In its September 17 announcement, Jones presents Quartz and Cranberry as their first real-world drinkable versions; Quantum is the returning flavor. The company also lists a Quartz-themed opener, collectible caps and a collector bag.

That makes the distinction between new packaging and new products important here. The news is not that every Nuka-Cola name in the box is making its debut. Two additions sit alongside a returning member of the range.

Where to look in the United States

Costco Business Delivery is a verified place to check: its listing uses item 2067687. The page warns that availability and pricing can change. We are not publishing a universal price or claiming that the item can be delivered to every ZIP code.

A retailer record is stronger evidence than an anonymous release calendar, but it answers a different question from whether your nearest store has the product today. Confirm the location and fulfillment option before planning a pickup or delivery.

Why we are not giving it one firm shelf date

Jones ties the limited pack to Fallout Day on October 23, 2026 and describes participating club-store distribution in the U.S. and Canada. Its release says late September shipping near the beginning, then gives September 11 near the end. Those dates conflict; we cannot resolve that from the announcement.

Shipping to retailers and arrival on a particular shelf are not interchangeable. The useful next step is a current store check, not treating either date in the release as a guaranteed nationwide on-sale day. The contradiction concerns timing, not whether the product has been announced.

How to read this status

We label this a confirmed release because a manufacturer announcement and a matching retailer record identify the same product. That is a different standard from a proposed flavor circulating in a rendering.

The label describes the product announcement, not a live inventory feed. It also does not mean the pack is a permanent addition to the soda aisle.

Before making a special trip or paying resale prices

Ask for the exact product name and item number, and check whether the store can verify physical stock. A search result can keep showing a product after an individual location sells out. There is no reason to assume a marketplace is your only option before checking the original retailer.

For a resale listing, compare the bottle count and accessories with the complete pack. A single bottle, an empty display box and an unopened assortment are different purchases. We have not taste-tested this release or assessed its future collectible value.
