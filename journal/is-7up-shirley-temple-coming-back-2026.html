7UP Shirley Temple is coming back. Is it staying this time?

The holiday soda has another return on the calendar. Here is what is confirmed, what to look for, and why a new bottle does not make it permanent.

By Discontinued Club ResearchChecked October 9, 20264 minute read
The short answer

Yes, a U.S. holiday return is confirmed. Permanent availability is not.

Keurig Dr Pepper included 7UP Shirley Temple in its 2026 holiday plans in February. An October 5 update again describes a limited-time holiday return and separately announces a new 20-ounce package for 2027. Those are two different pieces of news, not one nationwide on-shelf date.

When is 7UP Shirley Temple coming back?

The February 18 manufacturer announcement places the flavor in the nationwide holiday lineup for 2026. It does not give a specific first day in stores. That is the strongest basis for saying this year's return is planned, rather than inferring it from a social post or an old listing.

The October 5 update adds the 20-ounce format for 2027. If you are hunting for a drink now, do not read that future bottle announcement as proof that your convenience store has it already.

Our advice is to check the store you would actually visit, using the exact product name and package size. A national release plan helps answer whether a flavor is returning; it cannot answer which shelf in your town has been stocked today.

What flavor is Shirley Temple 7UP?

Target's regular 12-pack listing describes cherry and pomegranate with a citrus soda base. It identifies the drink as caffeine-free and lists 170 calories and 45 grams of sugar per 12-ounce serving. Those numbers describe that listed regular formula, not the Zero Sugar version.

For the right product, look for the full Shirley Temple name. A search for pink soda or cherry 7UP can lead to a different item. The photo above is an official product reference, not a photograph of a can we bought this week.

We have not taste-tested this return. There is no firsthand score here, and the available announcements do not establish that this year tastes different from the previous release.

Where should you look, including Zero Sugar?

Target maintains separate records for the regular 12-pack and the Zero Sugar 12-pack. The item numbers are 93054066 and 93054067 respectively. These are useful search references, not a promise of pickup availability.

Set a store or delivery ZIP code before relying on a stock message. During our check, the opened regular-can listing showed out of stock, although a search preview showed in stock. That discrepancy is a good reason to go past the preview and inspect the actual fulfillment option.

For a marketplace order, request a current package photo and check the printed date. A listing that predates this holiday season may still be selling earlier inventory. Its existence alone does not identify a newly delivered batch.

Is it discontinued between holiday releases?

Our classification is a confirmed seasonal return. That is different from declaring it permanently discontinued whenever local stock runs out, and different from promising it will remain on shelves all year.

A useful shopping rule is to separate the flavor, formula and format. Regular and Zero Sugar are distinct purchases. A future single bottle is a different format from a current multipack. An unavailable size does not settle the status of every version.

We would look for an explicit year-round commitment before changing the status to permanent. For another holiday soda with its own evidence and schedule, see our Coca-Cola Holiday Creamy Vanilla report.

How to read this status

Confirmed refers to the announced return, not nationwide real-time stock. We have not found a precise 2026 first-sale date or an end date in the announcements cited here.

Before you place an order

Frequently asked

Shirley Temple return questions

Is the 20-ounce bottle arriving in 2026?

The October announcement places that new package in 2027. It should not be used as a 2026 shelf-date claim.

Is there a Zero Sugar version?

Yes. Target has a separate Zero Sugar 12-pack record. Verify the formula and local availability on the actual listing.

Does an out-of-stock listing mean it was canceled?

No. A retailer stock message alone does not override a documented return plan.
