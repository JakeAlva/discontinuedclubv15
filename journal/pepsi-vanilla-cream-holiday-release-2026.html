Pepsi Vanilla & Cream launches for the holidays

A new holiday cola joins the seasonal soda hunt. Here is when to look for it, which formats are announced and what remains unconfirmed.

By Discontinued Club ResearchPublished October 19, 20263 minute read
The short answer

Yes, this is an announced U.S. release.

Pepsi has confirmed a limited holiday launch. The release details below come from the company, not a social-media mockup.

Release date, flavor and package sizes

PepsiCo's October 19 announcement says nationwide availability begins today, for a limited time. The cola combines vanilla and cream flavors, with Original and Zero Sugar versions in 12-pack cans and 20-ounce bottles. The company positions it as a holiday release, rather than a permanent addition.

We have not verified which nearby stores have received it. An announced rollout and an individual store having a case ready for pickup are different things. Check a store-specific listing before making a trip, especially during the opening days.

Search the full name, not just Vanilla Pepsi

Use the complete Vanilla & Cream name when searching. Then check the front label, whether the listing is Original or Zero Sugar, and the pack count. A short search result title is not enough to identify what will arrive.

That is particularly useful if you are following several dessert-style sodas at once. Our separate Coca-Cola Holiday Creamy Vanilla report covers a different branded product. Similar names should not be treated as interchangeable recipes or as evidence that one is replacing the other.

For a side-by-side tasting, buy comparable serving sizes and label each sample clearly. We have not tasted this release and cannot rank it against another cola. Cream in a flavor name also should not replace checking the ingredient and allergen information on the actual package.

What to check before paying a launch premium

Start with ordinary local retail searches. For an online offer, compare the total delivered price and the number of cans or bottles included. Divide that total by the quantity before comparing a case with a single item; a lower headline price can hide a very different purchase.

If you only want to try a new flavor, buying the smallest suitable quantity can make more sense than paying shipping on a large bundle. If you collect packaging, ask for photographs of the actual item and its condition, not just the promotional artwork shown here.

We would not pay extra on the assumption that a holiday label guarantees future resale value. Limited availability describes a selling window; it does not establish rarity, collector demand or a dependable investment.

How to read this status

This is release news, not a discontinuation notice. We are not assigning an end date that the announcement does not provide. A later empty shelf would require a new check before we call the product finished nationwide.

What this article does not promise

There is no preorder or stock reservation attached to this story. We have not independently tested the drink, verified retailer-by-retailer inventory or established an official suggested price. Those gaps should not be filled with guesses.
