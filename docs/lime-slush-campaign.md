with no announced date. Neither the banner nor the article offers a purchase or

single-can product photo as a packaging reference. The desktop Red Bull banner

for Discontinued Club's upcoming reserved Alani Nu Lime Slush four-pack. Output
aspect ratio approximately 2.56:1 (2304 x 900 ideal). Input image 1 is the exact
product reference: preserve the Alani Nu Lime Slush can shape, bright green
printed label, white Alani script, turquoise bottom band, silver rims, actual
typography and artwork exactly. Input image 2 is ONLY an established site
composition reference: dark empty left area for HTML copy, entire product group
on the right, full-bleed integrated advertising art. Do not include Red Bull.
Compose exactly FOUR Alani Lime Slush cans standing upright in a compact subtly
staggered lineup, fully visible with tops AND bottoms, occupying x=55% to 94%,
y=19% to 83%. Keep them comfortably within frame, crisp and photographed, not
oversized. Background: premium commercial graphic-art direction in near-black
forest charcoal #081810 at the entire left 48%, changing on the right into bold
acid-lime screen-printed diagonal panels with a small icy aqua accent and subtle
real paper grain; clean flat graphic geometry, NOT a fantasy environment or an
AI-looking 3D set. Strong restrained natural product shadow grounds all four cans
on a real matte surface. Flashy lime advertising vibe, polished and tactile, not
generic e-commerce cutouts or white squares. The left 48% MUST remain empty dark
charcoal without any object or text so white HTML headline can be placed there.
No added words, no headline, no numbers, no badges, no watermark, no logos besides
the real can packaging. No invented labels, no fruit props, no splashes, no mist,
no lens flares, no glow balls, no gradient orbs, no bokeh. Preserve the exact
reference can artwork.
