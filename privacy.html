Privacy policy.

The information used to run the store, fulfill orders, answer messages, and keep checkout secure.

Information we collect

When you place a direct order, information may include your name, email address, phone number, billing details, shipping address, items purchased, order value, and transaction status. When you contact us, we receive the information you submit in the form or email.

The website also stores cart contents in your browser so items remain in the cart as you move between pages. Discontinued Club does not currently require customer accounts.

How information is used

  • Process payments, fulfill orders, provide tracking, and handle returns or support.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, chargebacks, and other security problems.
  • Respond to inventory questions, sourcing requests, and product tips.
  • Maintain business, tax, accounting, and transaction records.
  • Operate, troubleshoot, and improve the storefront.

Services that process information

Stripe

Direct payments are completed on Stripe-hosted Checkout. Stripe processes payment credentials and transaction information under its own privacy practices. Discontinued Club does not receive or store your full card number.

Netlify

The website is hosted on Netlify. Netlify may process technical request information needed to deliver the site and operates the website contact form.

Shipping carriers

Delivery information is shared with the carrier and shipping services used to purchase postage, provide tracking, and deliver the order.

eBay

If you choose an eBay listing instead of direct checkout, the purchase occurs on eBay and is governed by eBay's policies. Discontinued Club does not combine the website cart with an eBay transaction.

Sharing and retention

Personal information is not sold. It is shared only as needed to process and deliver orders, provide requested support, comply with law, protect the store and its customers, or work with service providers acting on the store's behalf.

Order and communication records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for fulfillment, customer service, fraud prevention, accounting, tax, legal, and recordkeeping purposes.

Your choices

You can clear the website cart by removing its items or by clearing site data in your browser. You may also contact us to ask about personal information connected to a direct order or message. Some records may need to be retained where required for transaction, tax, fraud-prevention, or legal purposes.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the contact page or by email to hello@discontinuedclub.com.

Payment security

Card details are entered in Stripe Checkout, not in a payment form hosted by this website.
