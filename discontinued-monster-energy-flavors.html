Monster Energy:
A flavor history.

From the first release in 2002 to the flavors that disappeared from U.S. shelves. The cans, the changes, and what happened next.

Latest changes ↓

U.S. history. Launches, old favorites and departures.

About the dates & photos

A documented-by year is not an invented launch or retirement date. Older products without a verified end date are labeled "Exit date unknown." Photos identify the actual flavor; packaging may be from a later year, as noted in the image credits. The original is here for context and is not discontinued.

This is an expanding archive, not a claim that every regional size or formula is included. Foreign-only products and other Monster Beverage brands are outside its scope. Reviewed October 9, 2026.
