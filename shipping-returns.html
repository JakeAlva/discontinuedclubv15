Store policies

Shipping and returns, without surprises.

How direct orders are packed, priced for shipping, delivered, canceled, and returned.

Shipping costs

We currently ship direct website orders to addresses in the United States. Shipping on orders below $100 is calculated from the estimated packaged weight of the items in the cart. The exact shipping charge is shown before payment in Stripe Checkout.

Direct orders with an item subtotal of $100 or more receive free standard shipping. Required sales tax, when applicable, does not count toward the $100 threshold.

Handling and delivery

Orders placed before 12 PM Central Time are prepared for same-day carrier drop-off whenever possible. Orders placed later, on weekends, or on postal holidays are generally prepared for the next available carrier day.

Same-day handling describes when a package is prepared and dropped off. It is not a same-day delivery promise. Transit estimates shown at checkout are carrier estimates and can be affected by weather, holidays, address issues, and other carrier delays.

Tracking is sent to the email address used at checkout after the shipping label is created. Buyers are responsible for providing a complete, deliverable address.

Returns and refunds

Return requests may be submitted within 30 days of delivery. Items must be returned in the same condition in which they were received, including original packaging, tags, seals, and included accessories when applicable.

  • Opened food, drinks, supplements, cosmetics, and other consumable or personal-care products cannot be returned.
  • For a change-of-mind return, the buyer is responsible for return shipping.
  • If the wrong item was sent or the item arrived materially damaged, Discontinued Club will cover the appropriate resolution.
  • Original outbound shipping is not refundable on a change-of-mind return.

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned item is received and inspected. Banks and card issuers may take additional time to post the credit.

Damage or order problems

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery if an order arrives damaged, incomplete, or different from the listing. Include the order number and clear photos of the item, packaging, and shipping label so the issue can be reviewed quickly.

Minor wear that was clearly shown or described in the listing is not considered shipping damage. Product photos and listing details remain part of the condition description.

Cancellations

Contact us as soon as possible to request a cancellation. An order can usually be canceled and refunded before it has been handed to the carrier. Once the package is in transit, the return policy applies.
